# Wild whole chilled trolled European seabass

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers wild Dicentrarchus labrax taken by England inshore artificial-lure trolling, individually hauled and unhooked, sorted, chilled on an ice bed, protectively presented and physically received as whole ungutted raw never-frozen chilled fish by the first fish-product recipient. Actual trip, state and receipt records establish eligibility. Capture is removal from the wild context, independent of natural growth and of subsequent chilling. No aquaculture stage or natural-feed credit is invented. The Seafish route source describes both natural bait and artificial lures and both ice and slurry: only the artificial-lure and ice-bed branches are selected here. Historical iced whole landings support context, not universal current practice.

Exclude farmed bass, other species as reference products, live ornamental or nonfood fish, natural-bait trolling, slurry preservation, gutting, filleting, freezing, cooking, processing of meat or roe, and activity after first physical receipt. Do not infer a killing method, legal grade, safe temperature/time, shelf life, welfare benefit, stock status, zero bycatch or environmental credit. Actual animal condition at capture, chilling and receipt is recorded; live at capture does not imply live at the chilled gate.

Trip batch production is linked to lot-specific sorting, chilling and dispatch; it is not continuous aquaculture. Physical quantities are reconciled in their unallocated ledger and normalized per 1 kg reference flow; output-allocation shares apply only to the separate LCA burden view. Every applicable Section 8 collection obligation resolves into zero, one or multiple separately identified actual exchanges. Unknown occurrence is an evidence gap, never a zero. Each instance retains its own native property/unit, Range U, supplier/destination and node protocol. U is an unestablished row/instance-specific upper uncertainty endpoint to be investigated from primary data; 0..U neither claims an empirical range nor sets a universal cap. Quantities come from records, not these provisional endpoints.

Transfer ledger: T1 target capture→sort; T2 other animals capture→sort; T3 incidental matter capture→sort; T4 retained target sort→chill; T5 chilled fish chill→present; T6 presented fish present→delivery; T7 protective carrier/remaining ice present→delivery; T8 remaining ice/reusable carrier chill→present. Each pair records one identical transferred quantity/state. Capture-related immediate releases and alternatives occur before those transfers; later alternatives belong to their own node. Product alternatives, retained stock and reusable returns remain intended product outputs, not automatic wastes. No temporary holding intervention is required; record actual waiting and chilled custody in the relevant node with bounded times and burdens.

Each node's supplies collection scope additionally admits actual received/opening/returned fish-stock instances: preserve species, origin, whole/ungutted/chilled/never-frozen status as applicable, count and body Mass/kg independently from other native-unit supplies. Pair them with the prior closing-stock or return-output id, state, quantity, time and owner, and carry inherited upstream burden without new wild removal. Stock is separate from T1-T8 and new capture. Purchased stock does not prove this selected capture route; missing origin, prior-state or burden evidence blocks finalization. Record actual zero, one or multiple instances, rather than inventing mandatory stock receipts.

Release collection obligations below describe measured release records. If an actual instance is calculated from fuel, concentration or other collection, its resolved exchange must separately declare the exact formula, factor/source, input records and uncertainty with calculated_value/calculated_from_collection metadata and its own node protocol. ## 8. Foreground Data Collection, Calculation, and Quality Rules

### Data Collection Protocols

All protocols retain unallocated actual native quantities for physical reconciliation. Divide physical transfer, stock, product and material-fate quantities by same-lot accepted reference mass A from cp_delivery without multiplying them by co-product burden shares. Separately attribute shared resource, emission, service and asset burdens once to their responsible node/period/output, then normalize that LCA view by A. Raw trip/batch quantities remain inspectable. Material, animal and pollutant instances are not aggregated across incompatible states/substances/units. Each protocol owns one node; paired transfers reuse one physical measurement with two custody confirmations. Each includes actual incoming stock count/body Mass/kg/state, prior closing/return pair and inherited burden where present. cp_capture pools compatible attributed trip events including failures; cp_sort reconciles each species/grade/destination and stock balance; cp_chill reconciles T4 to T5/alternate/waste/stock and ice to T8/melt/destinations; cp_present reconciles fish and package/ice ledgers and reuse shares separately; cp_delivery computes accepted_seabass as A/A = 1 kg for A > 0 and normalizes physical quantities and attributable LCA burdens in separate labelled views. Every service/event has one owner. Calculated release instances require separate explicit calculation/evidence metadata as specified above.
