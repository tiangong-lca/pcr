# Wild raw whole northern shrimps, never-frozen fresh or chilled

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR defines wild trawl-caught Pandalus borealis delivered as raw whole/head-on/native-shell food or food-processing raw material at its first producer physical handover. The state may be fresh without an actual cooling intervention, or nonfreezing chilled. It is not ready-to-eat certification. This semantic boundary is narrower than CPC 3.0 04351. The UN source provides classification only; DFO distinguishes shore processing from at-sea frozen outputs, which are excluded here. Historical FAO handling guidance supports only qualitative capture/sorting/washing/draining/chilling interfaces, not current quantities, operating limits, taxonomy substitutions or legal requirements.
