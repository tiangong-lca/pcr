# Wild live whole hand-gathered common cockles

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This methodology covers only wild common cockles (Cerastoderma edule) gathered manually from natural intertidal beds and accepted alive, whole native shell, at the first harvester physical handover before intentional purification. Raking, riddling and bagging are evidenced actual tasks, with access by foot, boat or vehicle as recorded. This is a food-chain intermediate, not a ready-to-eat guarantee. [nrw-dee-cockle-management; fao-bivalve-depuration-2008]

Exclude other clams/cockles/ark shells, cultivation or managed relaying/growout, mechanical hydraulic suction, pump-scoop, prop-wash and box-dredge collection; ornamental, dead chilled, shucked, frozen, cooked, dried, salted, brined or otherwise preserved output. An accepted CPC narrower edge is not coverage of the entire live/fresh/chilled wild multi-species leaf.
