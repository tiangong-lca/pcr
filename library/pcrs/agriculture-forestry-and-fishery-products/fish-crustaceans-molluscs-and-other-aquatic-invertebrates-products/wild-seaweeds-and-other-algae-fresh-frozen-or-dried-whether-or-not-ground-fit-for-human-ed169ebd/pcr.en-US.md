# Wild whole sun-dried handpicked dulse

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR guides production of foreground data packages for wild Palmaria palmata handpicked from exposed rocks, sun dried and presented as whole-leaf food through the first actual physical food-product recipient. The representative route is the Grand Manan/Bay of Fundy route described by McHugh (2003). Cultivated tank dulse and final flaked, ground, extracted, salted or cooked products require another product boundary. Sun drying is required; seawater holding before drying is conditional. The source narrative does not establish actual lot food fitness, current certification or recipient acceptance. [fao-dulse-2003]

The data producer must qualify actual food fitness and primarily human-food use for each reference lot. Historic food use of the species alone does not qualify a lot. Nonfood destination alone does not prove unfitness. Usable inferior material handed to another processing operation is separately identified; it is neither this whole-leaf reference nor automatically waste. No default drying duration, bag size, moisture content, fresh-to-dried factor or shelf life is prescribed.

## 8. Foreground Data Collection, Calculation, and Quality Rules

The following is the declared accounting method (`dulse-ledger-method`); it specifies future collection, not observed production quantities. Each protocol owns one real node and collects its own card records. For every actual exchange retain instance id, semantic card id, physical/material/substance identity, state, native property/unit, direction, source/destination, transfer id, trip/run/lot/period, quantity and uncertainty. Record active/proven bypass/unknown separately. For support/water/package/plant stocks retain opening, received new/reused, closing, transfers, returns, losses and inherited burdens. Record every intended output and reject/fate without assuming occurrence. Calculated instances retain formula, measured inputs, factors, factor source, unit conversion, coverage and `calculated_value` / `calculated_from_collection`; directly measured instances retain `foreground_record` / `collected_record`. A conditional card's instantiated evidence mode follows the actual method, never an undocumented factor. Each actual carrier remains in its verified native exchange unit; auxiliary MJ calculations are separate analytical fields, never substitute or additional carrier exchanges.
