# Wild raw whole chilled hand-jigged European squid

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This methodology covers only wild Loligo vulgaris captured by small-vessel hand-operated jigs in the eastern Atlantic or Mediterranean and physically accepted from the harvester as raw whole ungutted chilled, never-frozen product. Capture may retrieve living animals; the final reference lot is not a live product. The actual lot must retain its species and route identity through the first harvester recipient gate.

Exclude farmed squid, other species including L. forbesii and L. reynaudii, cuttlefish, trawled or mechanically jigged catch, frozen/thawed lots, headed/gutted/skinned/cut products, intentionally extracted ink, live reference lots and buyer post-acceptance processing. These define this narrower PCR, not the classification of excluded products. No bivalve depuration, hatchery, feeding/growth, pure-oxygen supply or automatic environmental benefit is implied.
