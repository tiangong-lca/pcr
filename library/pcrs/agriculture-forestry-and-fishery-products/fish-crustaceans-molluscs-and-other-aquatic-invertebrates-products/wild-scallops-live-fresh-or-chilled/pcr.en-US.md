# Wild live whole hand-dived king scallops

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers wild Pecten maximus collected by divers by hand from natural seabed, delivered alive and whole in the native shell at the first harvester physical acceptance before intentional depuration. The declared gate is a methodological subset, not a claim that every commercial operator sells at this gate. It covers actual attributable producer collection and pre-gate handling responsibilities; a future-treatment buyer can accept the raw live product.

Exclude other scallops, dredging/trawling/electrical collection, culture or relayed growout, intentional producer depuration or sanitary relaying before first handover, ornamental use, shucked muscle/roe-only, dead/frozen/cooked/preserved outputs. No ecological harmlessness, certification, ready-to-eat status, legal eligibility, universal size, depth, temperature, season or shelf life is asserted. Sources establish qualitative route existence, not amounts.
