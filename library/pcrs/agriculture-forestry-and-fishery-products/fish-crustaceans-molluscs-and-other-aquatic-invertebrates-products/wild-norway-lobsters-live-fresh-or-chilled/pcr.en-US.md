# Wild live whole creel-caught Nephrops

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers wild creel-caught Nephrops norvegicus, alive and whole in native shell, intended for food at the first evidenced producer physical handover. Actual limb/shell condition is declared; whole does not guarantee an undamaged specimen. Its live-maintenance boundary does not include intentional growout or fattening. Creel is a deliberate methodological scope limit, not a sustainability ranking.

Any included bounded live holding uses the evidenced seawater-based family; non-immersed/dry or mist holding variants require separate evidence and methodology review and are not covered here. Exclude trawl capture, all other lobster/shrimp species, aquaculture, ornamental use, tail-only or peeled/processed meat (a trade name such as scampi alone is not a product-state definition), dead fresh/chilled, cooked, frozen, thawed or additive-treated reference products. Actual excluded-state outputs are nevertheless accounted at their generating node as evidenced other intended product, waste, environmental return or unresolved destination, never silently deleted or accepted as the live reference. No food-safety approval, legal permission, mortality, shelf-life or biodiversity performance is asserted.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

The operating mode is trip/lot-based, with recorded creel deployment/haul, catch handling and actual pre-gate live-maintenance periods. Shared cleaning/changeover and service events have one owner and identified consumers; no event is copied into every node. Retain biological interactions, creel effort, habitat contact and lost-gear observations in their scientific native measures, not fictitious animal kg or claims of negligible impact.
