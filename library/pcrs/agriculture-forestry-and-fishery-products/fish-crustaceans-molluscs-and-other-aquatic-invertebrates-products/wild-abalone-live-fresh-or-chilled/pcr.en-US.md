# Wild live whole hookah-dived blacklip abalone

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers evidenced Australian natural-rocky-reef wild Haliotis rubra, hand-removed with an iron by a hookah diver, remaining alive and whole in its native shell at the first harvester physical recipient acceptance. NSW supports species and reef-diver capture; VFA supports the iron and vessel air compressor; Narooma provides first-party evidence that wild live in-shell goods exist. These sources supply no universal PCR yield, temperature, holding time, legal limit, allocation fraction or sustainability claim. Actual location, lot and operations require foreground records.
