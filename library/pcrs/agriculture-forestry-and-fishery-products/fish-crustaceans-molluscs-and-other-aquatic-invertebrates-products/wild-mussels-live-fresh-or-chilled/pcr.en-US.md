# Wild live whole boat-hand-raked blue mussels at first harvester handover

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Applies only to wild Mytilus edulis removed from natural beds by hand rakes operated from boats, delivered alive and whole in their native shell at the first evidenced physical handover by the harvester for the food chain, before intentional depuration, sanitary relaying or managed growout. The recipient may be a purification buyer; the product is not thereby certified ready to eat. The Conwy evidence establishes a real route and pre-purification interface, not universal local equipment dimensions, seasons, PDO status or ecological performance.

Exclude other mussel species, dredged/mechanized harvest, cultured or relayed-for-growout mussels, ornamental animals, dead chilled products, shucked meat, frozen/cooked/preserved output and a harvester route that intentionally depurates before its first gate. Do not silently truncate that latter route: it needs another reviewed scope. Initial surface cleaning, actual pre-gate destination grading, short live maintenance and protective presentation remain conditional responsibilities here. Evidence of an ambiguous immersion purpose is required before deciding inclusion.

Declare the wild natural bed, coordinates, date, tide/trip, boat and actual hand rake. Capture is an independent removal interface, not biological growout or cleaning. Record rake deployment, initial screening, retained target mussels, incidental organisms and sediment. Include attributable outbound, return, idle, access and gear maintenance, even if acceptance occurs offshore. Manual work neither proves zero fuel nor zero ecological disturbance.
