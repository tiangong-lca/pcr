# Own-account wholesale of supplier-finished uncoated annealed float glass sheets

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Applies to actual own-account B2B merchant service for original supplier-finished uncoated annealed float sheets, in actual original interleave/pack/rack configuration. Preserve original sheet and stack identity, geometry/count, glass net mass/density basis and gross cargo/pack/support distinction. Ordinary hold/handling leads to real buyer acceptance, without merchant fabrication or remediation.

Manufacturer material supports actual storage-condition and product-state questions (`pilkington-ats104`; `pilkington-float`), not universal numeric storage/geometry/density or chemistry, mandatory cleaning/processing or automatic recycling. Actual recorded condensation, abrasion, stain and breakage differ from measured material loss, receiver discard or direct species.

All original glass manufacture/annealing/supplysize operations and inherited interleave/pack upstream, including embodied actual lost commodity, stay outside this service. Before complete-chain use, link quantified actual original-gate assigned demand and exclusive real loss coverage once; service disposal/treatment remains actual and once.
