# Ambient own-account wholesale of plain gold-alloy finger rings

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR defines actual physical ambient own-account B2B wholesale of factory-finished plain unplated gold-alloy finger-ring bands without gemstones or nonmetal inserts. Receive and resell unchanged design/size/source-lot pieces at actual buyer acceptance,with native gram net alloy mass,actual fineness/assay basis/source,external pack tare and condition. No fixed purity,carat,metal value or ring weight defines one commercial service.

Exclude gemstone-set,plated/base-metal-clad/gold-filled or nonmetal-insert rings,other jewelry/watches,metal mining/refining/alloying/manufacture,merchant resizing/polishing/cleaning/plating/assay operations,retail and fee agency. Source assay records are data,not a new performed test. Actual additional treatment requires bounded review/full inventory; scratch,missing piece or price change is not automatic physical loss/waste/emission.
