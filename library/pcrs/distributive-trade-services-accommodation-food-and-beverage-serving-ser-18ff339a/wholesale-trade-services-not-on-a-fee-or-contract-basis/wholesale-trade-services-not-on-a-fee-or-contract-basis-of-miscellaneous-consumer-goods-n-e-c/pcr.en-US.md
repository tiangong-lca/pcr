# Ambient own-account wholesale of new unmounted passenger-car tyres

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers actual physical ambient own-account B2B wholesale of factory-finished new rubber pneumatic passenger-car tyres,unmounted on rims,uninflated and not road-used. Manufacturer/model/size/source-lot/date,actual composite tyre mass/material basis and pack presence/tare,condition and commercial buyer acceptance qualify the service. Complete tyre manufacture and inherited constituents are merchandise,not rubber ingredient consumption here.

Exclude used/retread/truck/motorcycle/bicycle tyres,tubes/rims,wheel/vehicle service,mounting/balancing/inflation,washing,dressing/repair/retreading/shredding,tyre manufacture/road use,active preservation,retail,fee agency,batteries and musical instruments. Actual damage/returns/incidents and physical fates remain recorded;any additional actual operation needs separately reviewed complete scope,not assumed zero.
