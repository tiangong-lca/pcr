# Ambient own-account wholesale of sterile unfilled needleless three-piece syringes

## 1. Scope and Applicability

For actual original factory-sealed sterile-barrier single-use UNFILLED three-piece polypropylene hypodermic syringes WITHOUT NEEDLES,owned/purchased/resold in ambient B2B physical consignments. Declare model/complete barrel-plunger-stopper-lube-ink/included BOM,capacity versus real contents,sterile-release/barrier/expiry/lot/packing/condition. This is neither a filled-drug/pump/needle product nor a merchant sterilization,opening,repacking,filling or clinical-use/testing route.
