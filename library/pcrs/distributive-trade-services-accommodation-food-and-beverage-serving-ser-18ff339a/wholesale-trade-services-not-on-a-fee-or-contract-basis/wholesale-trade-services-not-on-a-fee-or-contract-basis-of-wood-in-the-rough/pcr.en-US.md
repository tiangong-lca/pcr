# Own-account wholesale of fresh bark-on Norway spruce sawlogs

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Actual freshlyharvested BARK-ON NORWAY SPRUCE (Picea abies) SAWLOGS, prepared at the actual source gate and received for ambient own-account B2B stock/condition/handling and FINAL commercial acceptance. Exclude sawnwood, veneer/pulp/fuel/other species/loggrades, merchant felling/extraction/delimbing/bucking/debarking/sawing/kilndrying/preservation/repack/use, retail/feeagency/storage-only. Actual additional operations require route review. Incoming logs are distinct from sawnproduct: no sawn moisture/density/dimension/grade defaults.

This service add-on excludes the complete actual upstream preparation of logs, including the upstream burden of lost merchandise. A separately matched source-gate dataset and actual receipt-haul coverage must reach the receiving state without gaps or overlap. After exclusive terminal assignment and reconciliation of identical baseline coverage, ACTUALLY integrate required additional demand and missing stages once outside the service, with completed status, link, native quantity, timezone, source, owner and stage/loss coverage before complete-chain readiness. A material CPC 03111 roadside producer dataset is an eligible upstream input only when actual species, grade, state, gate and coverage match. Its reference of 1 m3 solid UNDER-BARK wood, ready for loading at the ROADSIDE, and its conditional completed-feed entry do not replace the final Count-based wholesale reference. Do not repeat growth, harvest or preparation or omit actual receipt transport beyond that gate. Count actual merchant handling and dispatch within the service boundary and receiver transport/treatment once. Source-to-receiving-site transport before receipt belongs to the matched commodity upstream system and must not be repeated in the service. Logs, bark and water remain nonexchange stock records; do not automatically infer growth, sequestration or decay emissions.
