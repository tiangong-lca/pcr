# Frozen own-account wholesale of original-bagged non-edible industrial tube water ice

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Original factoryCLOSED bags of TUBE WATER ICE,actual SKU/source/composition/lot,owned/purchased/resold B2B with ACTUALLY OPERATED FROZEN coldstore/reefer holding maintaining usable ice through final business acceptance. Native solid/retainedliquid phases,bag-netmass/tare,temp-time/criteria and cold-duty/refrigerant coverage qualify service. Exclude dryiceCO2/snow/blocksculpture,factoryRO/icegeneration,merchant crushing/deliberate melt-refreeze/rebagging,retail/agency and using merchandise as facility coolant. Real extra operations need separate complete review;no universal bagmass,size,purity,setpoint,FDA-IPIA/life/leak default.

Only NON-EDIBLE INDUSTRIAL tube water ice whose actual supplier SKU, market presentation and independent classification evidence confirm CPC 17400 is in scope. Edible ice and ice presented for food or beverage use (22270), and SKUs lacking that confirmation, are excluded. Industrial buyer or use, tube form, RO treatment or purity alone do not establish 17400. Obtain supplier market-presentation/non-edible evidence and a documented classification decision before admitting a consignment; generic supplier webpages are not actual SKU proof.
