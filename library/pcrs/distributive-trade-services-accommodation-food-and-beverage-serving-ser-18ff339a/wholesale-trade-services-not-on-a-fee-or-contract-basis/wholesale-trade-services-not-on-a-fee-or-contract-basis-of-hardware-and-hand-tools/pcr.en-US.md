# Own-account wholesale of chrome-plated steel nonratcheting combination wrenches

## 1. Scope and Applicability

For actual complete factory-finished chrome-plated steel NONRATCHETING combination wrenches in actual original supplier packaging, owned/resold through ambient physical B2B wholesale. Record actual single-tool SKU/size/form, tool-versus-set presentation/genealogy, steel/finish evidence, included components and net-gross-tare/condition/native quantity/time. A source model table is evidence for questions, not a universal product specification. Exclude raw steel/tool blanks, ratcheting/powered/torque tools or other hand tools, forging/machining/heat treatment/plating/polishing/repair/cleaning/repacking, product tests/tool use, retail, fee agency and storage-only services.

This physical service add-on excludes all actual complete tool/steel/treatment/coating/original-pack manufacturing including upstream associated with actual losses. Before complete-chain readiness separately quantify/link complete original finished supplier-gate demand in actual native unit/state through sourced conversion/exclusive terminal assignment and identical baseline stage/loss coverage once. Actual merchant physical operations and declared receiver transport/treatment count once. A tool piece/set, steel or coating kg, torque/rated standard or money cannot replace the counted commercial-consignment service.
