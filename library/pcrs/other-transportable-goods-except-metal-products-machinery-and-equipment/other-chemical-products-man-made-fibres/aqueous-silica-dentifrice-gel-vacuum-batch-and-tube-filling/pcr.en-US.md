# Aqueous silica dentifrice gel vacuum batch manufacture and tube filling


## 1. Scope and Applicability

Admit only a real whole aqueous hydrated-silica dentifrice gel for personal toothbrushing, made by batch liquid preparation, gum hydration and abrasive dispersion, vacuum mixing/deaeration, slow final surfactant addition, then filling and sealing retail-kind plastic/laminate tubes. The actual formulation, grade and acceptance specification must positively identify a ready-to-use oral gel; this is a manufacturing family, not an invented fixed recipe or a named factory LCI. The Silverson complete Issue14TA4 guide identifies this gel route and ingredient functions; the independent ROSS report corroborates dispersion, hydration, vacuum and gentle final mixing. Neither provides a site inventory. Exclude white calcium-carbonate abrasive pastes, powder dentifrices, denture fixatives, mouthwash, medicaments, whitening peroxide/reactive acid-base systems, solid tablets, soap, household scouring cream, perfumes and room deodorants. Intentional antimicrobial/therapeutic actives other than separately disclosed sodium fluoride need reviewed scope extension, never a clinical-benefit default.

This is a distinct personal oral-gel method. It does not repair the unresolved finished-personal-perfume reference identity in earlier research or convert fragrance concentrate into finished perfume. No whole35323 coverage or approved study is claimed. The primary controller accepts the narrower scoped library association; exact delivery qualification and serial landing are recorded separately.
