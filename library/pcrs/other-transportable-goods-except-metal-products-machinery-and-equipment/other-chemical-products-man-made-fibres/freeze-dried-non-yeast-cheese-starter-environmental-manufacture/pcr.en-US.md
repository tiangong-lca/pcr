# Freeze-dried non-yeast cheese starter manufacture and dairy-gate supply

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This environmental method covers actual whole freeze-dried non-yeast bacterial cheese starter, including its real protective preparation, carrier and residual moisture, ready for the declared direct cheesemaking use. Novonesis White Classic establishes a real commercial cheese-starter family and a freeze-dried option; its frozen option is excluded. It does not disclose that commercial product’s recipe, strain or package construction, and this method does not reconstruct its factory inventory. Actual nonpathogenic food-use identity, strain/grade certificate, supplied state and acceptance specification must positively match the study. Exclude vaccines, toxins, pathogens, clinical products, yeast, cell cultures, dead biomass sold as food/feed, media without the starter organism, frozen/liquid starter, and downstream cheesemaking. No clinical benefit, potency equivalence, safety approval or equal cheesemaking performance follows from a kilogram.

The whole environmental manufacture boundary includes real preparation units and their full linked burdens. It is a bounded candidate methodology, not a factory dataset, biological protocol or approval.
