# Turned wood-bodied hand-operated yo-yo toys

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Complete new hand-operated yo-yo toys with two turned solid-wood body halves, the actual axial joining arrangement and usable string, including all actually supplied permanent hubs/tubes/caps/axle and bearing when present. The reference terminal is the assembled playable toy at first complete quality release.

Exclude timber, separate body halves, loose hardware/string and incomplete kits as the terminal; integral one-piece carved bodies, plastic or metal main bodies, powered/electronic/light/sound toys, toy musical instruments, dolls, puzzles, ride-on toys and general sporting equipment are outside this selected PCR boundary. This selected route does not represent all other toys.

The complete Enpu product page establishes a commercial wood yo-yo terminal and a factory capability chain from raw material/sawing/turning through finishing, assembly, quality control, warehouse and loading. Its displayed capability stations are not proof that every station runs on every order. PSI gives a two-half brass-tube/hub/cap/axle/bearing/string configuration; Craft Supplies shows a hub-and-axle configuration without a separate bearing in its drawing. Keep these configurations distinct. This is a transparent technical synthesis across primary originals, not one contemporary SKU, plant recipe or measured order. Do not infer wood species from another maker, string fibre, hardware alloy/plating, adhesive or finish chemistry, electronics, counterweights, dimensions, mass, density, yield, operating setpoints, age, lifetime, certification or competition performance. Manufacturer example dimensions and assembly advice are configuration-specific evidence, not PCR defaults.
