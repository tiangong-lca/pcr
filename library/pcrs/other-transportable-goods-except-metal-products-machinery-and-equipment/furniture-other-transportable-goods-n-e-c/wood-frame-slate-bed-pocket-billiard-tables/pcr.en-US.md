# Manufacture of complete wood-frame slate-bed pocket billiard tables

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional method covers manufacture of one configured new complete wood-frame slate-bed pocket billiard table. The representative synthesis uses kiln-dried oak stock, supplied wood-backed machined slate panels, woven wool-nylon cloth, canvas-backed rubber cushions, leather pockets and actual permanent fittings. It is a transparent technical synthesis of primary manufacturing and construction descriptions, not a claim that all evidence describes one SKU, factory or order. It begins with real supplied materials/components and includes onsite wood sizing, planing, drilling/routing, joint/cabinet/leg/rail manufacture, sanding, actual stain/lacquer operations, complete assembly and unique first quality release. Supplier-made slate, boards, cloth, profiles and formulations carry their earlier manufacture once upstream.

Exclude composite-board playing beds, metal-frame designs, dining tables, loose slate/frames/rails/cues/balls as the reference terminal, video-game consoles, casino/arcade/bowling apparatus and other games without their own complete applicability evidence. Whole-category classification wording does not extend this selected manufacturing method to every 38590 article. Use and end-of-life are outside this manufacturing reference. Installation is later service only after real complete first acceptance; actual first assembly/fitting/tests/acceptance on site remain manufacturing with all real producer interfaces. The selected native finished-at-plant binding still needs actual compatible whole-gate evidence or stays on hold. Matched transport-disassembled supply is not an assumed complete table, extra kit purchase or new Q.
