# Exposed and developed 35 mm black-and-white acetate motion-picture camera negative

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This methodology covers one actual physical terminal: a complete exposed and chemically developed, fixed, washed, dried and cooled black-and-white motion-picture camera negative, without soundtrack, on grey acetate safety base. The selected stock is EASTMAN DOUBLE-X5222 in35mm DXN718 BH-1866 on-core format. Start with purchased complete unexposed coated film; include its actual camera exposure and dark handling, then continuous D-96/D-96R development, stop rinse using the actual final-wash diversion, F-5 fixation, countercurrent washing, drying, winding, inspection and first quality release. Sources kodak-doublex-2022, kodak-format-2026 and kodak-h24-negative support this transparent technical synthesis; they are not one factory or one contemporary order.

Select on-site bulk preparation of D-96/D-96R and F-5 from the individually supplied materials below. Select anhydrous potassium bromide, decahydrate borax, anhydrous sodium thiosulfate, supplied28% acetic solution, crystalline boric acid and potassium alum. Record actual specification/assay and batch mixing; no table dose, replenishment, exposure, temperature, time, density, silver fraction, thickness, mass, yield, energy, emission or life is an inventory default. Complete premixed commercial developer/fixer supply is a different procurement boundary: it replaces those ingredient purchases with one full supplied formulation and retains only separately purchased water/chemicals and real site work. Do not sum both.

The selected waste interface collects actual liquid/sediment consignments for compatible external treatment or recovery; no on-site desilvering, bleach, direct outfall or automatic credit is assumed. Include any real required treatment before claiming dataset completeness. Exclude latent unprocessed film as terminal, unexposed film, still photographic negatives, paper prints,16mm7222, positive D-97, reversal, colour ECN/ECP, digital DCP, magnetic tape, soundtrack manufacture and film coating on site. Do not force an incompatible actual route into this method. Subsequent scanning/printing/projection/creative services and archival use are separate functions; executed storage/handling/pack-out to actual dispatch remains in a separately reported service account.
