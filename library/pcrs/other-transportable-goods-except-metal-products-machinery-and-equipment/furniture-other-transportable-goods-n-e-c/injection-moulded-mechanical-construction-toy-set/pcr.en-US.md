# Manufacture of complete injection-moulded mechanical construction toy sets

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Manufacture one complete configured mechanical plastic construction-toy set, containing the actual specified interlocking play elements and actual assembled mechanical subcomponents, from purchased complete thermoplastic pellet formulations and explicitly identified finished component supplies. Include receipt/storage, conveyance, actual moisture conditioning, colour mixing only when separate, melt plastication, mould filling/packing, cooling/ejection, gate/flash trimming, controlled internal regrind, actual element decoration/drying, component assembly, counting/selection, functional instructions and whole-set inspection and unique first quality release. Sales-box/bag packing is included where actually performed but its material mass is excluded from net toy Q. Source: lego-whole-set-process; lego-material-purpose; lego-profile-manufacturing; ifc-plastic-products-2007.

This is a transparent technical synthesis of a manufacturer whole-set route and general non-reactive plastics/environmental guidance, with independent ILCD support only for conditional attributional modelling. The LEGO sources are one publisher, not independent factories or one shared contemporary SKU/order. Material-purpose examples show that ABS is not every element: HIPS, PA, arMABS, MTPO, PC, bio-PE, POM, PP, TPU, SEBS and TP are counterexamples to an all-ABS recipe, not required constituents. Actual grades, formulations and make/buy records decide each card. Manufacturer temperatures, pressures, cycle times, test forces/heights, constituent shares, piece counts and durability claims are not PCR defaults or certificates. No default density, set mass, yield, lifetime, age rating or emission factor is supplied. IFC potential-fume examples do not establish actual normal-operation molecule emissions.

Exclude electric trains/track-signal systems, electric motors, batteries, lighting, radio/remote control, powered vehicles, reactive thermoset principal routes, metal/wood principal construction kits, loose spare blocks as terminal, generic resin manufacture, injection-machine/tool manufacture as terminal, purchased complete-set repacking, puzzles, use and end-of-life. This narrower route does not establish applicability to the whole classification. Real additional polymers, supplied components, fuel, refrigerant, chemical, printing/adhesive, packaging, water phase and waste outlet must be added as individual evidenced flows before a concrete dataset can be complete; they cannot be hidden in other-material or zero buckets.

UN CPC3.0 explanatory notes38540 also include electric/non-electric toy vehicles and accessories, non-electric toy motors, recreational scale models and their kits, and recreational full-size reproductions; electric toy motors are excluded there. This method selects the evidenced construction-set/building-block route within that broader scope. The adjacent puzzles and other-toy categories do not replace complete construction sets. Source: un-cpc3-38540-scope.
