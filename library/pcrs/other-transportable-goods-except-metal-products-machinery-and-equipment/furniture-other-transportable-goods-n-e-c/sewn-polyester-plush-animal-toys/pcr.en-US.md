## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional candidate covers new complete sewn animal-character plush toys made from supplier-dyed/finished polyester plush fabric, actual polyester loose-fibre stuffing and real eyes/nose/thread/permanent labels. Foreground includes receipt, pattern/layout/cutting, shell sewing/turning, actual face attachment/embroidery, filling, closure/sculpting and full-toy first inspection/release; actual later packing/storage/handling/dispatch/rework is a separate original-cohort service account. It requires manufacture from those inputs.

This is a transparent technical synthesis: the complete Steiff manufacturing account supports the sequence and full toy terminal; Tom positively specifies polyester plush and polyester fill; Aurora independently separates inspection stages. Glued cardboard joints, wood wool/mohair/cotton and alternative eyes in the general account are neighboring routes, not a measured Tom SKU/factory recipe or mandatory BOM. Actual supplier/BOM evidence must establish polymer, recycled provenance, components and performed operations; no recycled share, fill ratio or process setting is prescribed. Sources: `steiff-manufacture`; `steiff-tom`; `aurora-quality`.

The CPC category also includes human figures, unstuffed/dressed toys and moving/voice mechanisms; this route does not cover the whole leaf. Exclude rigid plastic/ceramic principal bodies, principal non-polyester shell/filling, powered/battery/sound/light mechanisms, the glued articulated-joint principal route, ordinary bedding/pillow inserts and material/empty-shell/eye/nose-only terminals. Add every genuine extra atomic input; a changed principal route needs assessment in this PCR. Sources: `un-cpc3-notes`; `steiff-manufacture`.
