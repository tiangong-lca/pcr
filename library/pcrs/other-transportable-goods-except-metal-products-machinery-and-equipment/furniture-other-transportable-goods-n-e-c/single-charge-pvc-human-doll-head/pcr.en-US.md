# Single-charge electrically rotationally moulded PVC human-doll head manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Manufacture one actual specification of a new complete hollow PVC human-doll head component: receive whole supplied liquid plastisol, meter one charge, electrically heat a closed multi-axis rotating mould through gel/fusion, actually cool in the water tank, demould, actually trim and inspect the complete head to its unique first quality release as a separately supplied unpainted head without rooted hair. Include face features and any actual integral neck from the drawing; do not presume separate eyes, hair, print/coating, glue or connector hardware. Actual packaging, storage, handling, dispatch and later work keep a separate original-cohort service account. `piper-rotation`; `piper-plastisol`; `adrie-head`; `zhongyi-machine`

This is a transparent technical synthesis: supply evidence establishes the separately sold formed head; the processor describes closed-mould PVC parts; equipment evidence offers electric heating and water cooling. These are not one SKU, current order, factory or recipe. The patent includes single-layer context and a multilayer painted-head example with optional eyes/hair; it is not this configuration. Catalogue weight does not establish net head mass; equipment current/voltage and power/hourly-energy labels conflict, so no numerical parameter is adopted. No plasticizer identity/fraction, lead status, recycled content, resin grade, age, certification, dimensions/mass/density, temperature/cycle/hardness, life, emission factor or energy intensity is prescribed. `anderson-head-2007`; `adrie-head`; `zhongyi-machine`

Exclude whole dolls, nonhuman toys, resin/plasticizer/unformed mix, purchased-formed-head repacking, injection/blow/open-drain slush routes, multiple-charge/multilayer work, separate onsite compounding, gas/diesel heating, silicone/other polymers and terminals with separately fitted eyes/rooted hair/coating/connectors. Real departures require applicability assessment and a complete actual atomic inventory within this record; they never authorise deleting occurred facts. An unpainted head is a complete component supplied for later whole-doll manufacture, not an assembled doll; later customer painting/rooting cannot be retrospectively included in this configuration. Original UN p.192 includes heads/bodies/limbs and excludes separately supplied glass eyes; adjacent38520 concerns whole dolls and38540/38560 other toys. `un-cpc3-2025`; `adrie-head`; `anderson-head-2007`
