# Complete maple-mounted hand stamps with laser-engraved rubber faces

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This methodology covers one complete non-self-inking hand stamp: a solid maple block with a handgrip profile, a supplied double-sided self-adhesive foam cushion and a rubber face laser engraved/cut from a purchased complete cured sheet at the stamp plant. The maple mount itself is the grip; an additional knob is not part of this selected construction. The product is usable with a separate compatible ink-pad, which is not bundled here. A face, mount, ink-pad or image service alone is not the core product. The broad CPC leaf and native finished-article family also include other products; neither makes those routes equivalent.

The technical route combines positive whole-product and compatible separate process instructions from manufacturers. The sources do not represent one shared SKU, contemporary order or measured factory inventory. A site must verify which operations actually occur; purchased prepared mounts/sheets/cushion articles carry their upstream burdens once, while in-house laser removal, water cleaning, bonding, trimming and trial release carry actual local loads. Molded rubber, flash/photopolymer, pre-inked/self-inking, purchased-finished-die-only assembly, other mount constructions and separately sold ribbons/pads/printing sets need their own applicability review. No recipe, hardness, dimensions, item mass, temperature, time, laser power/speed, yield, ink dose, impression count, quality threshold, life, certification or emission factor is prescribed.

Route and construction sources: `rubberstamps-wood-anatomy`; `rubberstamps-wood-product`; `trotec-stamp-engraving`; `trogroup-laser-rubber-vulcanisation`; `cape-arago-mounting`; `simply-stamps-manufacture`.
