# Complete straight auto-opening steel umbrella frame manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR addresses one independently complete straight auto-opening steel umbrella frame before canopy and handle assembly. It includes the actual steel shaft, main ribs and stretchers, moulded top notch and runner, operating spring/catch and joint hardware, matched steel ferrule and actual supplied metal rib tips. Matching loose tips are part of the controlled whole-frame supply when declared; they do not make a separate reference output. It does not combine single ribs, handles and frames into one terminal. The selected foreground route receives already formed and surface-finished components, performs real inspection/sorting and any necessary fit/deburring, rib-to-stretcher and runner/notch riveting, wire retention, spring/catch assembly, motion/lock inspection and actual rework, then required supply packing and one unique complete-frame first quality release ready for supply at the plant. Upstream component forming/tempering/coating is carried once in the bought component datasets. This is substantive complete-part assembly, not purchase and repacking of a completed frame. `sep-complete-steel-frame`; `acme-frame-structure`; `zhe-frame-riveting-2026`

Septrainbow positively offers a complete steel frame with steel shaft, runner, tips, top/ferrule and auto-opening state. Its images show a bare assembled frame and separate finished tips; the light-coloured runner photo and transparent-runner text do not establish a polymer specification. Acme independently describes channel-steel ribs, notch/wire and runner assembly before handle attachment. ZheBrella describes actual steel-joint riveting, holes/finishes and pre-canopy motion checks, while also discussing incompatible fibreglass/hybrid alternatives. The 1970 official investigation describes separately traded assembled frames and their distinction from finished umbrellas; its historical prices, sizes and production rates are not current inventory data. These are a transparent qualitative technical synthesis across sources, not one contemporary SKU, factory, order, recipe or performance certification. Gold colour or the phrase gilt coated does not establish elemental gold, zinc, nickel, coating thickness or the chemistry of this frame. `usitc-umbrella-parts-1970`; `sep-complete-steel-frame`; `acme-frame-structure`; `zhe-frame-riveting-2026`

The original CPC leaf also covers other umbrella and walking-stick/whip accessories. This narrower frame method does not claim their coverage. Exclude raw steel strip/wire/tube as terminal, isolated ribs or handles, wood and injection-moulded-handle manufacturing routes, fibreglass/carbon/aluminium/hybrid principal frames, furniture/jig frames, bought-complete-frame repacking, onsite component coating/heat treatment, canopy cutting/sewing/printing, parent umbrella assembly, use and end of life. A real additional operation or configuration requires source and exchange-specific applicability review within this same PCR identity. No numerical dimensions, mass, density, alloy, formulation, loss, yield, energy, temperature, time, life, strength, corrosion, opening-cycle or certification threshold is prescribed. `un-cpc3-2025`

| a_causal | all inventory rows | For each remaining exchange identify the determining physical causal relationship to its co-functions, supported by the real process response.
