# Solid mango-wood and natural-rattan kitchen storage cabinet manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers a new complete rigid indoor kitchen storage cabinet with a load-bearing solid mango-wood body and genuine natural woven-rattan detailing in wood-framed fronts. Art & Craft Furniture documents Alvara AR06/A as a kitchen cabinet with a solid mango-wood frame, authentic rattan and one-piece delivery. Its image shows enclosed storage, open shelves and drawers. These observations establish a real whole-article subtype, not its factory BOM, joint design, finish recipe, dimensions for other products, load rating, lifetime or a universal kitchen-cabinet construction. Actual complete supply includes the body, back, shelves, all supplied doors/drawers and each actual supplied fitting; household contents, room installation and subsequent use are outside the first-release reference event.

The production boundary is a transparent technical synthesis, not a claim that Alvara uses KLEIBERIT or BEHR. It starts from burden-bearing kiln-dried solid mango timber and genuinely completed supplier fronts, then actual body sizing/planing/joinery/boring/sanding, one-component aqueous PVAc cold bonding, a qualified untinted waterborne polyurethane/acrylic clear finish brushed onto wood, actual drying, final fitting and first-release acceptance. EPA documents purchased dried lumber, assembly before or after finishing, adhesive joining, machining, sanding and coating. The declared bounded variant procures completed wood-framed natural-rattan door and drawer-front assemblies; their weaving, treatment, framing, backing, finish and drying are retained in real complete upstream producers. Body drying is upstream only when actual purchased dry timber and its complete provider support that gate. No prefinished-part receipt or packing activity substitutes for the real body manufacture and whole completion.

Separate whole-waste physical disclosure Qt/Qanalysis from linked modelled attributable treatment demand or burden vector A/Q0. Retain measured physical component/cohort provenance where actually known, and separately evidence the service allocation in units compatible with the actual provider. A synthetic accounting test, not empirical data or a default: one actual 10 kg filter export containing 8 kg substrate and 2 kg retained material serves new and old cohorts. ### Process: Original-cohort factory service (`cohort_service`)

Maintain three separate accounts: complete physical receipts, transfers and opening/closing raw/WIP/finished-goods/asset stocks in their actual units; the fixed first-acceptance area Q0; and attributable environmental exchange/burden vectors A by original cohort. For a manufacturing cohort include own opening inherited foreground vectors and all actual period loads, remove only closing unfinished/unassigned vectors and evidenced transfers to independent functions/cohorts once. Closing accepted FG is not deducted as unfinished WIP. Upstream provider aggregate inventories and their separately decomposed histories are alternative representations, never added twice. Rework loops, failures, samples, idle and zero-output periods keep their real inputs, loss and treatment; internal transfers cancel only once on consolidation. Separate whole-waste physical disclosure Qt/Qanalysis from linked modelled attributable treatment demand or burden vector A/Q0. Qanalysis identifies the disclosure study only; it never assigns all shared waste to each original cohort. Retain measured physical component/cohort provenance where actually known, and separately evidence the service allocation in units compatible with the actual provider. Shares must be supported, non-overlapping and exhaust the assigned service; unknown shares remain unresolved. A synthetic accounting test, not empirical data or a default: one actual 10 kg filter export containing 8 kg substrate and 2 kg retained material serves new and old cohorts. Evidenced 25%/75% treatment service produces 2.5/7.5 kg-equivalent demands, and Qnew=100 m2, Qold=200 m2 gives 0.025/0.0375 kg-equivalent per m2. The receiver still gets one 10 kg waste; neither 2.5 kg physical export nor a repeated 10 kg demand is valid. Apply this distinction to shared packaging waste only when its actual shared recipient/service history exists. Keep full physical old/new equipment, installed filters, fluid and tool stocks, purchases, replacements and retirement separate from their attributable service vectors. The complete filter fleet and old cohorts cannot disappear because the studied cabinet is new. No default lifetime, replacement rate, annual utilization, machine mass or common burden fraction is imposed. Actual retired tools/assets and their attached contamination are exported whole once; nested composition and provider-service allocation do not manufacture extra physical exchanges. Each actual lubricant, refrigerant, replacement part, cleaner and energy carrier gets its own atomic card before use; unknown chemistry is not represented by a pure constituent proxy.

### Process: Packaging physical custody (`packaging_custody`)

Maintain three separate accounts: complete physical receipts, transfers and opening/closing raw/WIP/finished-goods/asset stocks in their actual units; the fixed first-acceptance area Q0; and attributable environmental exchange/burden vectors A by original cohort. For a manufacturing cohort include own opening inherited foreground vectors and all actual period loads, remove only closing unfinished/unassigned vectors and evidenced transfers to independent functions/cohorts once. Closing accepted FG is not deducted as unfinished WIP. Upstream provider aggregate inventories and their separately decomposed histories are alternative representations, never added twice. Rework loops, failures, samples, idle and zero-output periods keep their real inputs, loss and treatment; internal transfers cancel only once on consolidation. Separate whole-waste physical disclosure Qt/Qanalysis from linked modelled attributable treatment demand or burden vector A/Q0. Qanalysis identifies the disclosure study only; it never assigns all shared waste to each original cohort. Retain measured physical component/cohort provenance where actually known, and separately evidence the service allocation in units compatible with the actual provider. Shares must be supported, non-overlapping and exhaust the assigned service; unknown shares remain unresolved. A synthetic accounting test, not empirical data or a default: one actual 10 kg filter export containing 8 kg substrate and 2 kg retained material serves new and old cohorts. Evidenced 25%/75% treatment service produces 2.5/7.5 kg-equivalent demands, and Qnew=100 m2, Qold=200 m2 gives 0.025/0.0375 kg-equivalent per m2. The receiver still gets one 10 kg waste; neither 2.5 kg physical export nor a repeated 10 kg demand is valid. Apply this distinction to shared packaging waste only when its actual shared recipient/service history exists. Keep full physical old/new equipment, installed filters, fluid and tool stocks, purchases, replacements and retirement separate from their attributable service vectors. The complete filter fleet and old cohorts cannot disappear because the studied cabinet is new. No default lifetime, replacement rate, annual utilization, machine mass or common burden fraction is imposed. Actual retired tools/assets and their attached contamination are exported whole once; nested composition and provider-service allocation do not manufacture extra physical exchanges. Each actual lubricant, refrigerant, replacement part, cleaner and energy carrier gets its own atomic card before use; unknown chemistry is not represented by a pure constituent proxy.

Separate whole-waste physical disclosure Qt/Qanalysis from linked modelled attributable treatment demand or burden vector A/Q0. Qanalysis identifies the disclosure study only; it never assigns all shared waste to each original cohort. Retain measured physical component/cohort provenance where actually known, and separately evidence the service allocation in units compatible with the actual provider. Shares must be supported, non-overlapping and exhaust the assigned service; unknown shares remain unresolved. A synthetic accounting test, not empirical data or a default: one actual 10 kg filter export containing 8 kg substrate and 2 kg retained material serves new and old cohorts. Evidenced 25%/75% treatment service produces 2.5/7.5 kg-equivalent demands, and Qnew=100 m2, Qold=200 m2 gives 0.025/0.0375 kg-equivalent per m2. The receiver still gets one 10 kg waste; neither 2.5 kg physical export nor a repeated 10 kg demand is valid. Apply this distinction to shared packaging waste only when its actual shared recipient/service history exists.

Dust control captures are not emitted dust. Preserve chips, shavings, collected dust, substrate/retained-contamination composition, fugitive dust and actual outlet particulate mass as distinct quantities without double counting. Any filterable stack test covers only its stated sampling train and operating window; it does not establish total fugitive, condensable or other unmeasured material. Waterborne finish is not zero-VOC or zero-hazard. Actual coating/glue/wood/cleanup compounds, water phases and carbon origins require applicable validated identification/quantification and uncertainty; add each genuinely present elementary species as its own row. Total VOC, an SDS list or a mass-balance residual cannot invent a molecular species, natural-air composition or unmeasured zero. No control efficiency or emission factor is assumed.
