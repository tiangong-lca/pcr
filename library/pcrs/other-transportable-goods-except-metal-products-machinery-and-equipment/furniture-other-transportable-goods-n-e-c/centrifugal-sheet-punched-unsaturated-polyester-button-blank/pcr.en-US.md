# Solid polyester button blanks by centrifugal sheet casting and punching

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers a complete solid nonfoamed, unreinforced and unpigmented unsaturated-polyester button blank supplied for subsequent button turning/drilling. Select one documented route: supplied liquid resin system → centrifugal sheet casting → punching → actual hardening/cure/conditioning → first blank quality release. A blank is the finished traded component of this foreground, not a finished sewing button. The public sources establish complementary product and process facts; their technical synthesis does not assert one common SKU, order or factory. Acceptance limits, ingredients and all quantities come from the actual producer/buyer records.
