# Single-trunk maple stump table manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional method covers complete indoor occasional stump tables whose load-bearing body is one unjoined maple trunk section, shaped as a square-section block, with a qualified clear sealant finish and actual nonstructural nylon floor glides. Receive the actual rough-cut maple section; retain all real cutting, debarking, conditioning and kiln drying; shape and sand the same body; mix and spray the qualified clear waterborne two-component furniture coating; dry and sand as required; fit the actual glides and their actual fixings; inspect the complete table and record its unique first acceptance. The reference product includes the whole wooden body, finish, glides and all actual BOM accessories. A log, board, tabletop, seat component or purchased finished table is not this manufacture.

Bertu Home's Square 1218 is positive physical evidence: it is sold as a stump table under Stumps & Side Tables, is made from a solid rough-cut maple trunk, and is hand-cut, debarked, kiln-dried, sanded and sprayed; the complete article has nylon glides. The same source explicitly permits use as a stool or grouping as a coffee table, reports size variation, cracking, humidity movement and later refinishing, and offers pigmented colours. These facts remain part of applicability. Admit this table method only for an actual declared occasional-table configuration and use; an exclusively seating, office or bedroom study needs its own applicability decision. Do not pretend that a multipurpose article has only one possible function. The related quick-ship bowtie product explicitly adds joints to limit cracking; that joined construction is excluded. Sources: `bertu-square`; `bertu-bowties`.

This is a transparent technical synthesis, not an assertion that the cited manufacturers share a SKU, plant or recipe. Bertu supplies the complete physical terminal and manufacturing operations, but does not identify its kiln heat source or sealant formulation. This selected route uses an electric dehumidification kiln with real electric auxiliary heat, documented refrigerant system and condensate/vent interfaces, and qualified two-component clear waterborne furniture coating. USDA and Nyle document the drying technology and large-section drying difficulties; ADLER documents the selected finishing technology. No Nyle lumber schedule is transferred to a thick trunk. Retain actual species/section-specific drying schedule, independent surface/core moisture and drying-defect tests, coating-substrate permission and finished-table acceptance. Ordinary source examples of dimensions, price, time, moisture, load, formulation ratio, spread, energy, mass and lifetime are not defaults. Natural, black and whitewash are not interchangeable coating recipes.

Exclude multi-member glued or veneered bodies, panels, bowtie repairs, separate structural legs/frames, seats supplied solely for seating, cabinets, office desks, bedroom furniture, components, customer-assembly kits, resin-filled cracks, pigmented/burnt/oil/UV/solvent-borne principal finishing, onsite fuel combustion, gas-fired or steam/hot-water drying, purchased finished-table repacking and hidden outsourced processing. Other actual principal routes require an independent applicability review and full actual atomic inventory. Natural cracking is not a zero-loss assumption or evidence that any damaged body passes. Packing, storage, handling, shipment, returns and actual post-acceptance work remain separately observed original-cohort services. Upstream forest/log preparation, every supplied article and external utility retain their real producer burdens once.

## 6. Process Inventory Structure

Every card retains its complete description plus applicable §§1–5 and 7–9. M/S in records are manufacturing/service account labels, not mass values. Collect undivided raw quantities; cards and protocol aggregation reference the single calc_quotient calculation. Gross physical V_i remains independent of LCI burden vector A_i. Physical-only gross cards do not purchase, characterize or credit a flow; a separate charge/service card owns a real upstream burden once. Conditional absence requires evidence. The first-release reference is an internal manufacturing-to-FG transfer: it cancels on consolidated physical gates, where opening/closing FG and real G/R replace it, never add beside it.
