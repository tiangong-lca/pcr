# Optically exposed but undeveloped black-and-white silver-gelatin polyester sheet film

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method produces an identified physical latent-image article: purchased complete ILFORD HP5 PLUS black-and-white silver-gelatin negative sheet film on polyester support in the nominal 5x4-inch format, optically exposed, unloaded and protected in a black light-tight bag and a reusable sheet-film box, then first released while still chemically undeveloped. Manufacturer material guidance, documented 4x5 exposure practice and the historical HARMAN box/bag transfer configuration are compatible evidence for this technical synthesis; they do not identify one contemporary order or one factory. Actual cut contour, lot, packaging construction and release criteria must be collected. Sources: `harman-hp5-2018`; `ilford-sheet-exposure-2018`; `harman-sheet-transfer-2016`; `ilford-gelatine`; `cci-latent-image`.

The foreground starts at receipt of finished unexposed sheets with their upstream provider. It includes storage and any actual acclimatisation, dark loading, orientation/holder checks, metering and controlled optical exposure, closing and identifying the exposed holder, dark unloading, traceability/integrity checks and protected first handoff. The latent image is invisible: acceptance uses validated exposure-operation and chain-of-custody records, not an invented visible-image density test. Actual rejected attempts, start/stop, idle, cleaning, tests and rework remain in the inventory. Any real destructive quality test requires its complete actual test stage and atomic exchanges; it never turns the reference article into a developed negative.

Exclude base or emulsion manufacture from this exposure foreground; represent it once in the whole-film provider. Unexposed stock is an input, not the reference output. Developed negatives, motion-picture products, paper prints, instant/reversal/chromogenic routes, cameras, exposure equipment and creative/photographic-service functions are not this terminal. No developer, fixer, wash bath, steam boiler, compressed-air system or emission is assumed to exist. Actual additional operations and individual exchanges must nevertheless be included and applicability reassessed. No clinical or industrial-radiation operating parameters, universal exposure setting, storage lifetime, thickness/density, silver content, yield or operating intensity is prescribed.
