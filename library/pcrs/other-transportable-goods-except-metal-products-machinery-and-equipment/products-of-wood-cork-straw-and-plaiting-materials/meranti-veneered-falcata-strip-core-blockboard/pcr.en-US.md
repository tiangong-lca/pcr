# Five-layer Meranti-veneered Falcata strip-core blockboard with UF veneer bonding

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Applies to unfinished interior five-layer blockboard made by bonding four Meranti veneer sheets around one purchased, dried, edge-jointed solid Falcata lumber-strip barecore. The two Meranti crossbands are perpendicular to the core strips; the Meranti outer face and back are parallel to the core. The complete ply axes are 0°/90°/0°/90°/0°. Each veneer is an unbonded sheet no thicker than 6 mm. This is a solid-strip core, not a fifth veneer sheet. Both actual outer plies must be verified tropical Meranti; a tropical core or trade name alone is insufficient. Sources: `cbp-five-ply-blockboard-2011`, `qld-meranti-timber`, `fao-farm-structures-1986`.

CBP's retained sample description identifies an edge-jointed Falcata lumber core and two Meranti veneers on each side, with crossed successive grains and unfinished outer plies. It establishes a real construction, not this PCR's adhesive recipe, modern tariff treatment, or a CPC correspondence. Queensland's timber guidance identifies Meranti as tropical South-East Asian timber with Shorea/Parashorea names; USDA distinguishes Meranti groups and variable properties. Actual suppliers must resolve botanical identity, accepted trade group, origin, legal/provenance evidence and every layer's role. The sample dimensions are not prescribed defaults. Sources: `cbp-five-ply-blockboard-2011`, `qld-meranti-timber`, `usda-wood-handbook-2021`.

The selected foreground begins with the purchased barecore and dry veneers. It covers conditioning, recipe preparation from dry UF resin, dry ammonium chloride and separately metered water, crossband/outer layup, hot pressing, trimming, sanding, acceptance and PE-film packing. It is the declared UF route, supported as a technical option by UNIDO, not an assertion about the CBP sample manufacturer's glue. Supplier core assembly, its actual edge adhesive, lumber drying and veneer production are upstream; their complete burdens and material constituents travel with the purchased inputs. Grid medium-voltage electricity and externally supplied net hot-water heat serve this route; there is no foreground fuel combustion. No filler, overlay or coating is included; an actual additional chemical requires its own atomic row and applicability review. Sources: `unido-panel-adhesives-1977`, `frihart-wood-joining-2023`.

Exclude all-veneer tropical plywood, coniferous-faced blockboard, non-tropical non-coniferous faces, three-layer blockboard, two solid-core-layer five-ply boards, laminboard, MDF/particle/cellular cores, bamboo, densified wood, structural glulam/LVL, coated panels, exterior/marine performance claims, integrated core-making/green drying and alternative skin adhesives. No empirical yield, dose, density, moisture, strength, lifetime or emission range is transferred from another route.

The CPC 3.0 31422 association is a candidate narrower interpretation whose acceptance is held by the repository owner under docs/adr/2026-11-10-hold-blockboard-cpc-associations.md. The actual four-Meranti-veneer/one-solid-Falcata-core construction remains the semantic product boundary. Official 31412/31413 notes expressly include blockboard and permit a central core of another material, while 31422/31423 also describe crossed multi-veneer conditions. A solid-strip core and the solely-sheets branch title do not alone exclude 31412 or settle 31422. The prior batch-03 acceptance is historical; the current positive mapping has no edge for this code. Manufacturer construction and native UUID evidence are not official classification rulings. No replacement classification, whole-leaf coverage, independent scientific approval or publication is asserted. Sources: `un-cpc-notes-2025`, `un-cpc-explanatory-notes-xlsx-2025`, `un-cpc-introduction`.
