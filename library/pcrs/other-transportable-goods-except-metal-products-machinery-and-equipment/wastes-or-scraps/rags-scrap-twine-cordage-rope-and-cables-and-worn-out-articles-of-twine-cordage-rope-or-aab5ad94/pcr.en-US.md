# Dry-cut coloured cotton-rich T-shirt wiping rags

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR covers one physical supply: cotton-rich cut wiping cloth made from one segregated grade of previously washed coloured knitted post-consumer T-shirts that is no longer suitable for direct reuse as clothing. Cotton-rich is a supplier/receiver specification to verify by actual fibre-composition testing, not an assertion of pure cotton or a numerical cotton-content threshold. Keep every measured co-fibre, retained dye/finish and water fraction. The cloth structure remains intact; this is neither fibre garnetting nor a waste-treatment service. The receiver accepts a usable wiping Product against declared size, cleanliness, absorbency, lint and metal specifications.

The foreground starts with received unwearable uncut garments, their prior history and inherited supply burdens. It includes reception, dry composition/condition grading, removal of hard accessories and unsuitable areas, actual powered cutting/conveying, metal detection, inspection and first quality release. Subsequent compression/packaging, storage, handling and dispatch stay in an original-cohort service ledger. This is a transparent technical synthesis: LMB describes unwearable clothes and a clean/dry cotton-rich sorting/cutting/metal-detection route; SJS independently confirms grading and inspection but explicitly includes washing and sterilising. The latter is counterevidence to treating all rag production as dry. Select this dry branch only with actual records confirming no on-site wet treatment, bleaching, sterilisation or heated drying. Prior supplier cleaning is disclosed and carried upstream; it is not assumed absent or free.

The native broad rag Product identity is used only for this single qualified cut-cloth exchange, never as a selector among rags and ropes. It specifies manufactured finished product and at-plant production mix, with no fixed cotton grade, CAS, geometry or geography. Its Chinese general comment uses an imprecise hemp/cord wording while its bilingual baseName and English description include rags; this discrepancy does not establish hemp composition. The electricity identity below is limited to Chinese grid-average consumption below 1 kV; it is a conditional modelling interface, not a claim that the UK LMB or SJS factory uses that provider. No plant quantities, default yields, purity, package mass or environmental superiority are inferred.

The LMB coloured T-shirt grade supplies the single representative form. Its product text says washed T-shirts and cautions that bag attributes vary; it does not locate the washing step. This method selects already washed raw T-shirts at reception and requires their actual upstream wash burden/history. If washing occurs inside the chosen foreground, retain it in full and reassess the dry-branch scope. Advertised cotton/absorbency/lint percentages, cloth dimensions, pack weight and item count are supplier claims, not adopted quantities or a universal quality standard. Other catalogue grades are excluded from this reference.
