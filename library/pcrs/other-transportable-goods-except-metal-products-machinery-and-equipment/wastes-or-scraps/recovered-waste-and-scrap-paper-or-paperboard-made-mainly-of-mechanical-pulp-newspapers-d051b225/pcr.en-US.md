# Dry-sorted baled sorted clean news for repulping

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional PCR supplies one dry-sorted, wire-bound newspaper feedstock grade for subsequent repulping: ReMA Sorted Clean News (SCN), item 58, with independently established mainly mechanical-pulp newspaper origin. It starts at each original holder’s actual source-separated release/collection interface, inherits the applicable source-paper history, and includes collection-point work, road collection, receiving/weighing, inspection and dry sorting, removal of wrong grades/non-paper objects, electric conveying, hydraulic compression, wire binding, identification, first quality release and attributable finished-stock storage, handling, returns/rebaling and loading through the ex-works gate. This is a recovery-material supply method. Its reference function is not a waste-treatment service or purchased finished-bale packing service.

SCN is sorted newspaper stock from source-separated programs/drop-offs/paper drives or comparable identified source streams. Ordinary newspaper sections and original inserts may remain in proper proportions; excessive ink, brown grades and non-paper objects must be controlled. For the selected SCN specification, prohibitives are at most 0.5%, outthrows plus prohibitives at most 1%, and other acceptable papers at most 10%. The preamble separately prohibits any medical, organic/food, hazardous, poisonous, radioactive or toxic waste and harmful substances/liquids under zero tolerance; the 0.5% ceiling cannot authorize them. Outthrows are papers unsuitable for the specified pack; other acceptable papers require buyer acceptance. These are nested grade limits, not additive recipe fractions or a claim that every newspaper has a fixed fibre composition. Record actual fractions and the governing buyer/seller agreement, including any requirement to exclude flexographic inks. ReMA’s preamble specifies dry packing and a 12% maximum moisture unless an alternative and its test are agreed before shipment. Measure actual moisture and its basis; do not use 12% as a recipe or normalization factor. CEPI’s EN-related 10% commercial moisture convention is countercontext and is not combined with SCN or used to erase physical water. [rema-scn-58; cepi-recycling-qc]

UN 39243 is a furnish-based category for recovered paper/board made mainly of mechanical pulp. SCN alone does not establish that condition. Source-paper records or suitably validated furnish identification must establish it; colour, printing, whiteness and classification labels alone do not. This method covers the narrower newspaper SCN grade, not all journals, magazines, unsold-news grades or graphic-paper mixtures. Exclude 39241 unbleached kraft/OCC as reference supply, 39242 mainly bleached chemical-pulp cuttings/office-paper supply, 39249 other/unsorted paper, virgin newsprint, new paper manufacture and already repulped/deinked slurry. Allowed residual constituents in the selected whole grade are disclosed, not independently purchased components. [un-cpc-39243; rema-scn-58; cepi-grade-countercontext]

The representative foreground is a sheltered dry yard with electric sorting/handling and an electric hydraulic baler using purchased whole mineral hydraulic fluid, steel wire and whole printed tie-on paper tags. The sequence is a transparent synthesis of compatible primary evidence, not a measured contemporary factory or a single-order/SKU claim. Bollegraaf documents dry separation followed by baling/shipment for reuse; HSM corroborates compression and wire binding, without making its cutting-scrap case this newspaper facility. No wet pulping, washing/deinking of the paper, bleaching, intentional thermal drying, onsite combustion or steam boiler is assumed. Actual different operations, water, fuels, detergents, additional packages and individually identified releases must be added with their real boundaries; they cannot be hidden in a zero or an “other” exchange. No factory yield, density, energy intensity, historical-burden percentage or recovery credit is supplied.
