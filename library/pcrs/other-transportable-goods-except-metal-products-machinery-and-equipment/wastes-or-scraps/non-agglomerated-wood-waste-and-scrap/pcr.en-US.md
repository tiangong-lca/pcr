# Non-agglomerated green Nordic spruce sawdust from sawmilling

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers one physically segregated loose sawdust stream generated by sawing untreated green Nordic spruce sawlogs into lumber, including the source joint sawing operation, necessary separation and first quality release for subsequent use. The product is fine kerf sawdust at natural measured moisture, supplied in bulk without industrial thermal drying, binder, chemical treatment or pressure agglomeration. A declared acceptance specification must distinguish its actual sieve distribution and particle morphology from coarse sawmill chips and planer shavings; no universal sieve limit or moisture number is invented. Ordinary conveying or temporary bag containment is not agglomeration. The target is the sawdust subset of UN CPC 3.0 39283, not every non-agglomerated wood waste form (`un-cpc3-2025`).

The technical synthesis combines a real Nordic-spruce sawmill/by-product context (`metsa-renko-sawmill`), a separate primary wet-sawdust supply and coarse-chip distinction (`sodra-sawdust`), and independently checked sawmill/wood measurement methods. It does not assert a single supplier, current order, plant recipe or measured performance common to these sources. Their numerical moisture, energy, ash, density, yield and allocation examples are not defaults. A concrete dataset must prove its own spruce identity, segregation, untreated state, moisture and supply gate.

Exclude pellets (39281), briquettes and other agglomerated forms (39282), wood flour deliberately milled to filler grade, coarse chips, flakes/planer shavings, bark products, mixed hog fuel, virgin lumber as the reference product, post-consumer wood, MDF/OSB or other glue-bonded composite residues, painted/preservative-treated or otherwise contaminated wood. Do not blend these into a future selector. Another species, dried-sawdust route, chemical contamination, on-site fuel-powered equipment, boiler or dryer requires explicit scope and additional atomic inventory assessment before use; an actual route difference cannot be denied by a zero row.

### Stage gates and physical reconciliation

The source chain is sawlog receipt → actual debarking → sawing/edging/trimming → segregated fine-sawdust collection → any installed contaminant screening/aspiration → first release Q_c → storage/temporary containment → bulk loading/dispatch. Keep independently measured co-product cuts for green lumber, coarse chips and bark; they do not become alternative reference products. Recovered fine sawdust returns only when the actual acceptance specification permits it. Repeated passes consume real energy and resources. Actual boiler/thermal drying/chemical treatment is a scope change, not a silently omitted stage.

For each measured component k (dry wood, bark, ordinary water, each auxiliary constituent and any independently measured relevant element), the production gate is opening WIP_k + fresh inputs_k = closing WIP_k + new release_k + joint outputs_k + production waste_k + production emissions_k. Release water and dry mass have their own measured state. Do not add closing FG to this equation because released FG is already inside the new release term. The service gate is opening FG_k + release_k + external returns_k = closing FG_k + gross shipments_k + service waste_k + service emissions_k. Cancel the common release transfer to obtain the combined gate; preserve the same physical terms and stock states. For true transformations include independently evidenced reactants/products (including oxygen or reaction water if real) before attempting closure; this electrical mechanical route invents no chemical reaction. A residual is a disclosed gap with uncertainty, never two fitted unknowns or an automatic gas.

For each packaging article p, use opening empty + opening attached WIP/FG + opening reusable stock + new purchases P_p + first external nonpurchase physical receipts N_ext,p + true external returns received ER_p = closing empty + closing attached WIP/FG + closing reusable stock + packaging accompanying gross physical shipments S_p + external return EO_p + waste W_p. P_p, N_ext,p and ER_p are mutually exclusive for each receipt. N_ext,p includes first borrowed/leased or supplier-attached components genuinely not acquired with a purchased consignment; an unitemized component actually acquired within that consignment remains P_p. ER_p requires the same component's documented previous outward dispatch. Add each actually missing nonpurchase receipt as a specific atomic material/form/type input interface with measured raw quantity, receipt/custody/cohort and owner/inherited-vector/provider evidence; the purchase-only card remains purchase-only. Physical receipts and stocks do not by themselves create a new manufacture purchase or burden. Keep one real owner and provider contribution once, including existing unused-stock history and only actual additional services; no whole-load duplication, credit or new Q_c. Use the same physical mass basis and deduplicate overlapping stock locations. Internal reuse/transfers cancel; they are not new P_p, net product Q_c or an automatic co-product. For bulk supply S_p may be measured zero, but temporary service containers, their wear and external movements remain accounted. A new term is allowed only for a real additional event; remove a proven duplicate from the same side rather than invent a compensating opposite-side flow. Whole mixed wastewater/oil/packaging exports and composition subaccounts are non-additive representations of one physical exchange and one actual provider.
