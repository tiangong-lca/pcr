# Formation and titanium-reuse supply of Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 wet bar-turning chips

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional PCR covers one segregated new machining-scrap stream: Ti-6Al-4V Grade 5 chips genuinely formed by wet finish turning of source-certified mill-annealed solid circular bar. Follow whole-stock receipt and inherited burdens, actual machining with its mother workpiece, separate chip capture, only necessary documented draining/cleaning/foreign-object removal, testing and first quality release to a named titanium-reuse receiver specification. Keep actual packing, storage, handling, transport and receiver receipt in the original cohort service accounts. This is not purchased-finished-chip repacking.

Taylor et al. provide direct bar-to-chip wet-turning evidence, including dry and other-alloy countercases. Weston et al. demonstrate graded/cleaned Grade 5 swarf supplied for reuse but do not disclose the cleaning recipe; one interstitial specification passes while another is exceeded. Titanium Industries corroborates alloy/grade/type-segregated scrap reclamation. These independent sources support a transparent qualitative technical synthesis, not one contemporary factory, SKU, order or guaranteed receiving approval. No fixed coolant concentration, bar dimension, cutting speed, cleaning chemistry, dry state, yield, density, energy intensity or recycling credit is adopted.

Exclude all other metals in 39367, mixed grades/origins, Grade 2 and Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al, titanium-bearing steel, tungsten-carbide tooling as reference scrap, TiO2, sponge/primary unwrought metal, powder, plate/tube machining without its own reviewed stock route, post-consumer material, electronics, sludge/ash, smelting/refining, FAST consolidation, atomisation and finished recovered metal. A receiver’s feed approval is specific to its actual recovery route; no general remelt or aerospace-material qualification is inferred.
