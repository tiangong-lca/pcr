# Source-segregated Sn-Sb-Cu pewter casting gates for remelting

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This attributional PCR covers one source-segregated solid metallic gate/runner stream from genuine manufacture with one identified lead-free, tin-predominant Sn-Sb-Cu pewter alloy. Start with receipt of whole pre-alloyed notch bars or ingots and their upstream burden history; include actual electric melting/holding, mould preparation, centrifugal casting, cooling/demoulding, hand clipping of the casting ring, separate collection, necessary dry foreign-object removal, weighing/assay and unique first quality release to an identified remelting receiver specification. Retain actual packing, storage, handling and delivery through the receiver gate as original-cohort services. Buying already formed scrap and repacking it does not fulfil this scope.

ASL and Nathan Trotter establish real tin-base Sn-Sb-Cu casting feed; T&T establishes an actual centrifugal-casting and hand-separation operation; Nathan Trotter and DKL establish scrap receiving/recycling and analysis functions. This is a transparent qualitative synthesis of compatible primary operations, not one SKU, plant or contemporary order. The cited alloy examples differ. Each package must identify its one real alloy lot, mould construction, power/holding configuration, actual contamination state and receiver agreement. No example composition, temperature, density, yield, recycling efficiency or historical zero burden is adopted. Sources: asl-pewter; tt-casting-2012; nt-britannia-2015; nt-white-metals; dkl-reception.

Nicem and Contenti technical documentation independently supports compatible electrically heated, temperature-controlled tin-alloy melting and holding equipment for this bounded spin-casting route. This equipment-level support does not establish that ASL, T&T or the modelled source site uses a particular furnace. The actual alloy, heater/holding configuration and operating records must be verified for each package. Sources: nicem-f100-spin-casting-furnace; contenti-electric-melting-furnace-manual.

Exclude tinplate/steel cans, tinned copper or brass, copper-base bronze, lead-base solder/type metal, zinc-base white metal, intentional precious-metal alloys, whole solder paste/flux preparations, solder-use residues, oxide-rich dross/ash/filter dust, mixed grades/origins, post-consumer articles, finished cast articles, remelted refined-metal ingots and a waste-treatment-service reference. A small Sn content, colour or trade name never establishes tin-base identity. Dross generated by this route is inventoried separately and never relabelled as this metallic reference product. A different alloy or melting/forming/chemical cleaning route requires evidence and inventory expansion before use. un-cpc3-2025.
