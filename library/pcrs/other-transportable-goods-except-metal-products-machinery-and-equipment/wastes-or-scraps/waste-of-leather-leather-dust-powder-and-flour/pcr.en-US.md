# Wet-blue bovine leather trimming feedstock at receiving plant

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR is a bounded technical synthesis for one supply form: moist undyed unfinished chromium(III)-tanned bovine edge trimmings generated by actual trimming of purchased wet-blue hides and supplied separately to an accepting leather-fibreboard plant. It includes original formation, segregation, necessary physical preparation and receiver delivery once. It is not a record of one factory, SKU, current order or measured industry factor. The method is limited to attributional studies requiring partitioning.

Do not pool wet trimmings with thickness-leveling shavings, buffing dust, powder/flour, untanned or limed hide pieces, wet-white/vegetable-tanned scraps, finished leather offcuts, leather articles, fibreboard or treatment services. The literature distinguishes these sources and their carried chemicals. Chromium-bearing status, actual biocides, contamination, legal waste/by-product classification and receiver acceptance must be established for the actual lot; neither a material UUID nor a historical reuse example establishes an exemption.
