# Untreated used Injekt/Sterican venous blood-sampling sharps

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This PCR retains the Other clinical waste identity and covers one narrowly defined clinical material: the used connected Injekt Luer Solo 2 ml REF 4606027V and Sterican standard 21 G x 1.5 inch REF 4657527 assembly generated by adult diagnostic venous blood sampling. It is not a selector for mixed hospital waste. The original polymer/metal/coating assembly and actual retained blood remain together; collection-container mass is separate. The rule is a transparent technical synthesis of the identified manufacturer documents and WHO practice/management guidance, not an observation of one hospital or one contemporary procurement order. No composition fractions, residual liquid amount, device kg/item, density, pathogen threshold or default burden share is supplied.

Do not substitute unused devices, pure PP waste, recycled granules, separated steel or treatment services. Pharmaceutical waste (39931), municipal waste (39910), mixed sharps, detached needles, other syringe materials/configurations and chemically/thermally treated material are outside this reference. Actual clinical practice, legal classification, operator permissions and receiver acceptance require current local primary evidence; this PCR grants none.
