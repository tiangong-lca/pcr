# Cupola-melted phenolic-bonded stone-wool insulation board manufacture

## 1. Scope and Applicability

Applies to whole uninstalled, unfaced and uncoated asbestos-free stone-wool thermal-insulation boards made from actual natural basalt-based mineral feed and compatible actual secondary mineral material, through coke-fired hot-blast cupola melting, short-fibre formation on rotating wheels, aqueous phenolic binder application, mat formation, gas-fired drying/curing, cooling, actual cutting/edge trimming and final quality inspection to unique first release. Actual retained cured binder, dust-suppression oil, adhesion additives and residual water belong to the whole board. Real crushing, grading, purchased or actual onsite recycling briquetting, cooling/cleaning/controls, failed production and conditioning are included when performed. This is manufacturing from real feed, never purchased-finished-board repacking.

The complete JRC industrial document establishes the cupola/short-fibre/cure route and alternatives; the complete manufacturer EPD establishes real resin-bonded whole-board terminal and aqueous binder/oil application. Together they form a transparent technical synthesis, not a claim that one SKU, factory or contemporary order demonstrates every generic operation. The EPD covers several shapes and2022 weighted German-site data; this method selects bare boards and a declared cupola route without claiming the German boards used that furnace. Neither source supplies this dataset's recipe or quantities. JRC Table2.9 is final oxide context, not a purchase recipe; glass-wool water circuits and gas/electric melting alternatives are counterexamples. Historical emission tables omit startup/shutdown and contain mixed denominators or equivalent indicators; do not transfer their numerical yields, energy, factors or species into first-release Q. [jrc-glass-bref-2013; rockwool-medium-epd-2024]

Exclude continuous basalt reinforcement fibre; E-glass roving/fabric/mat; glass wool; high-purity ceramic/AES refractory wool; foam glass; moler brick; raw basalt/mineral powder; worked mica/graphite/peat products; binder-free or inorganic-binder boards; electric-arc/flame-melter variants; oxygen-enriched melting; faced/coated/laminated panels; compressed coated facade panels; loose/blowing wool; rolls/pipe sections; installed exterior wall or other building systems; onsite resin synthesis without separate full chemical-process coverage. Another actual route/product requires renewed complete applicability; never omit actual inputs to make a route fit. UN37990 is broader than this one product/method. Scientific approval, accepted mapping and publication are separate from authoring. [un-cpc-2025; jrc-glass-bref-2013; rockwool-medium-epd-2024]

This declared unit measures manufacturing supply only. It sets no operating recipe, furnace/cure temperature/time, speed, fibre dimension, test threshold, conductivity/fire/acoustic/strength rating, density, yield, energy, service life or certification default. The EPD's volume/nominal-density convention, infrastructure exclusion, waste assumptions and module-D benefit are not adopted; use measured same-configuration geometry/mass and the limited attributional hierarchy below. [rockwool-medium-epd-2024; jrc-ilcd-2010]
