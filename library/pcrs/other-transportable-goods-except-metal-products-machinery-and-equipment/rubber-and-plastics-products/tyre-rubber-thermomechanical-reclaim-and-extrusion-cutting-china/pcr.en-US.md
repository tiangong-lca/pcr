# Thermomechanical tyre-rubber reclamation, extrusion and cutting in China

## 1. Scope and Applicability

This method covers whole tyre-derived reclaimed rubber that undergoes real thermomechanical plasticization, cooling extrusion, die formation and cutting in China before first acceptance. It is not unchanged screened tyre powder, pyrolysis carbon black, new NR/SBR compounding or a finished revulcanized article. The actual 2025 manufacturer report calls its grade liquid reclaimed rubber and describes a hot viscous phase, a second cooled extruder, a sheet-forming die and automatic cutting before bagging. Preserve those facts together. Admission requires the actual output to be an extruded sheet or strip uncut or simply cut to rectangular (including square) shape under the Chapter40 form terms and cut to its declared configuration at the at-plant gate; unformed bulk liquid cannot inherit that reference. A sheet-head equipment name alone is not final-geometry proof: actual formed-section observations, dimensions and packaging-state evidence are required for the chosen grade. Measured section dimensions and specific supplied formulations are actual study/reference-point admission data; this methodology supplies no universal dimensions, mandatory factory SKU or equivalence of every phaseIII liquid grade. No cold self-supporting state, common patent/factory, fixed recipe, operating condition or present legal/quality approval is inferred.

## 9. Validation Rules

This method covers whole tyre-derived reclaimed rubber that undergoes real thermomechanical plasticization, cooling extrusion, die formation and cutting in China before first acceptance. It is not unchanged screened tyre powder, pyrolysis carbon black, new NR/SBR compounding or a finished revulcanized article. The actual 2025 manufacturer report calls its grade liquid reclaimed rubber and describes a hot viscous phase, a second cooled extruder, a sheet-forming die and automatic cutting before bagging. Preserve those facts together. Admission requires the actual output to be an extruded sheet or strip uncut or simply cut to rectangular (including square) shape under the Chapter40 form terms and cut to its declared configuration at the at-plant gate; unformed bulk liquid cannot inherit that reference. A sheet-head equipment name alone is not final-geometry proof: actual formed-section observations, dimensions and packaging-state evidence are required for the chosen grade. Measured section dimensions and specific supplied formulations are actual study/reference-point admission data; this methodology supplies no universal dimensions, mandatory factory SKU or equivalence of every phaseIII liquid grade. No cold self-supporting state, common patent/factory, fixed recipe, operating condition or present legal/quality approval is inferred.

The whole reference Product is Semi-finished rubber parts / 半成品橡胶部件, with a Mass reference, China production-mix and calendering or extrusion and cutting hard fields. Its complete comment explicitly covers sheets/profiles cut to size; it does not fix a polymer purity, virgin-only supply or vulcanization state. Preserve native CPC36220 as countermetadata. The admitted reclaimed grade must independently meet the actual source, phase, whole composition and cut-geometry conditions; a label alone cannot override native fields. The input Product Rubber Powder / 胶粉 identifies screened scrap-tyre powder before reclamation, not the final transformed product; its native CPC36113 countermetadata and at-plant context remain visible. Keep residual fillers, carbon black, oils, water and all actual constituents within each whole supplied material. Neither a pure polymer nor a component UUID substitutes for the filled reclaim.

Include actual pitch receipt and storage, weighed mixing, preheating, screw-driven thermal/mechanical reclamation, cooling/degas extrusion, sheet die, cutting, bagging, handling and quality release, including startup, failure, idle, rework and cleaning. The source material table and process diagram name tyres and tall-oil pitch, while generic equipment/process text also mentions powder/liquid aid stations and grade-dependent additions. Preserve that limitation: equipment labels do not establish chemical use or absence, and the report is not a complete transferable formulation. Obtain the actual whole supplied formulations, supplier/lot and composition for the chosen grade; every actual additional chemical needs its own concrete atomic card before a study is complete. Do not invent powder ingredients, a powder-free recipe, pure-chemical substitutions, dose, yield or universal operating settings.

Fix first acceptance after the actual cut configuration, documented grade/quality release, whole-product net weighing and declared packing configuration. Independently sum unique first-accepted whole wet masses into Q and lock each original cohort.
