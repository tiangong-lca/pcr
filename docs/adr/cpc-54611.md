CPC 3.0 54611 is **Electrical wiring and fitting services**. Accepted relation is `narrower`: this PCR owns first installation of one indoor non-electronic 15 A, 125 V grounded duplex receptacle, with side screw termination to compatible prepared copper conductors in an existing suitable box, plate fitting, testing and signed handover. It does not cover every wiring/fitting service in the subclass.

The UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes, PDF page 293, include basic wiring/fittings and distinguish fire alarms (54612), burglar alarms (54613) and telecommunications wiring (54619). Evidence: https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf . The Leviton installation sheet and 5320 product information support the selected separate hot/neutral/earth side-screw fitting route and actual technical acceptance boundary. Actual delivered-model instructions and jurisdiction/design control suitability; no universal mass or installation quantities are inferred.

The accepted object is the complete first-installed single-feed grounded fitting and plate at signed installer handover. Branch-circuit/box/panel construction, replacement/removal, split-feed and push-in methods, electronic/GFCI/AFCI fittings, alarms, telecommunications, appliance fitting and later electricity use are excluded. A prepared interface is disclosed, not a product credit.
