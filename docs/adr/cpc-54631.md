# CPC 3.0 54631 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

Official leaf: **Heating equipment installation services**. The [UNSD CPC Version 3.0 explanatory notes, 30 June 2025](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), PDF page 294, include installation of heating equipment and related pipe/duct/sheet-metal work, central-heating controls, district-heating connection and domestic-boiler/burner maintenance. The same page separates space-heating apparatus from water plumbing and heating installation from general electrical installation. These broader inclusions do not become obligations of this selected narrower product category.

Canonical target: `pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.construction-services.heating-equipment-installation-services`.

Proposed relation: **narrower**. A first fixed installation of a Class I resistive wall panel convector is a heating-equipment installation service, but does not cover the full subclass. This is an individual draft decision, not an accepted edge or a claim of complete leaf coverage.

## Semantic boundary

The input gates are a prepared accepted wall, an existing suitable isolated fixed electrical connection point, a packaged Class I resistive panel convector with its manufacturer-matched H-frame and fitted lead, and actual additional fastening/connection components. The service includes receipt checks, actual drilling or verified preprepared holes, secure upright mounting, hook/clip retention, competent fixed connection and protective-earth/electrical verification, control initialization, limited functional heat test, cleanup and signed acceptance of one installation.

Class II appliances, new branch circuits or supply upgrades, wall strengthening, boilers, heat pumps, hydronic/distribution networks, freestanding equipment, replacement/repair of an existing heater and normal heating use after handover are excluded. An unsuitable supply or wall is a scope failure. The category does not claim lifetime delivered heat or building thermal performance.

The [Glen Dimplex PLXE Series C long manual, Issue 7](https://www.dimplex.co.uk/sites/g/files/emiian551/files/media_import/medias/docus/13/PLXE%20-%20Series%20C%20Long%20Manual%20-%20Issue%207%20%283%29.pdf), PDF pages 3 and 7-10, establishes an illustrative Class I fixed-supply/earth and matched-bracket route, orientation/locking, first-power setup and possible initial heat-up odour. It does not establish generic installation material quantities, electricity consumption, pollutant identity, emission factors or allocation methods. Own foreground protocols establish job accounting, acceptance records, returns, sharing and direct-release evidence needs.
