## Semantic boundary

- Included products and market state: Actual physical own-account wholesale service for already wet-salted untanned bovine hides; monitored operated ventilation, contained drainage, stock rotation and condition inspection through a completed B2B accepted consignment. Actual hide count, grade and salt/moisture/drained mass are qualifiers, not interchangeable units.
- Excluded adjacent products: Animal/slaughter/flaying/initial curing; new re-salting/brining, soaking/fleshing/production trimming/tanning; dry, non-bovine or tanned leather merchandise; retail, fee agency, pure financial title transfer and storage without purchase/resale. Any actual product-changing operation requires explicit scope review, not deletion from inventory.
- Production route and handover gate: Purchased already-preserved hide receipt -> inspected and actually controlled warehouse holding -> contractual business-buyer accepted resale. Condition failures, rehandling, returns and actual reject/drip/loose-salt/cleanup exits remain owned and physically reconciled.
- Why the relation is supported, and any partial-coverage limit: Bovine raw-hide resale fits hides/skins/leather own-account wholesale, but this PCR covers only an explicit wet-salted bovine, physical warehouse-intervention subset. The raw bovine hide producer/curing PCR owns a commodity origin/removal/preservation gate and does not replace this additive trade service. All merchandise upstream production including loss fractions is excluded here but quantified original-gate lost-commodity adjustment and saleable burden must be linked exactly once in the commodity chain. Wet brine/salt mass is not collagen or original fresh-hide mass.
