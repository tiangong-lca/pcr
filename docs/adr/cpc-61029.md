# CPC 3.0 61029 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

Official leaf 61029 is wholesale trade services, not on a fee or contract basis, of food products n.e.c. The [UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), printed page 301, include group 237 macaroni, noodles, couscous and similar farinaceous products. The author independently extracted and read that page from the official original PDF. Proposed target `pcr.own-account-sealed-dry-durum-pasta-wholesale` is narrower: manufacturer-sealed plain dried durum-semolina pasta, ordinary ambient merchant own-account wholesale to an actual business-buyer accepted commercial gate.

## Semantic boundary

One Count reference is the actual unique completed commercial service, qualified by supplier, finished dried/packed release, SKU/shape/source lot, pack-unit count, actual net pasta mass, moisture basis/method/source/sample/time, inherited packing/support tare, actual product date type/value, condition history, periods, merchant ownership and buyer criteria. It is not one package, pasta kilogram, invoice, cooking portion or monetary margin.

Exclude grain/semolina/flour manufacture and pasta mixing/forming/drying, fresh/egg/stuffed/cooked/hydrated products, noodles/couscous/dishes, re-drying, product chemical treatment, retail repacking/resealing, dedicated climate intervention without review, fee agency, retail and standalone storage/transport. Ordinary ambient storage and inherited manufacturer drying/seal do not establish a new merchant preservation intervention; four system-condition modules address consignment mode, crossed periods, actual rejection/return/breach and full-life shared infrastructure.

The closest uncooked-pasta producer directory was read in full and is an empty scaffold with title only. It supplies no material methodology to reuse. Existing merchant siblings do not provide this product's moisture-basis/as-received/dry-matter distinction, date/shape/pack condition and finished-pasta release demand/loss duties. Independent physical merchant methodology, rather than a classification gap, justifies the semantic target. No existing product prose was copied.

All traded-pasta and inherited-pack production burdens, including losses embodied upstream, remain outside the service add-on. The independently linked original gate must cover grain cultivation, milling, pasta manufacture/forming/drying and inherited packing/final release once; raw grain or semolina alone is incomplete. Actual source-gate input and assigned demand include evidenced original material and ordinary moisture/state-change share with compatible unit/basis/source/allocation/conversion, while returns/closing/other exits are assigned exclusively. Actual compatible loss adjustment, owner and existing-loss coverage are reconciled once outside service. Final dispatch plus discarded dry material cannot silently replace original-gate demand; wet-weight deficit does not prove dry-pasta loss, waste or species.

Actual package tare, net as-received pasta and wet-basis versus dry-basis moisture assay are distinct nonadditive views. Useful broken pasta, rejected intact packs, actually collected loose pieces/fines, separated packing, mixed cleanup solids/liquids and direct released species have different states and receivers. Whole intact waste cannot also transfer the same contents and packing as duplicate outputs. Captured/swept fines are not automatically airborne dust. Direct species/receiving medium and factor/composition/fate need independent evidence. Service cleanup and receiver treatment retain one actual owner.

[Barilla's storage FAQ](https://www.barilla.com/en-us/help-with/storage-reheat-and-product-faqs/pasta-and-pasta-sauce-storage) and [product FAQ](https://www.barilla.com/en-us/help-with/storage-reheat-and-product-faqs/general-product-faqs) were read directly as primary manufacturer technical material. They support qualitative dry-storage/product-date context and the durum/semolina/manufactured-pasta distinction. Consumer cooked-food, sauce, portion and numerical practices are not universal wholesale instructions, moisture benchmarks, loss factors, shelf-life or safety guarantees. Generic inventory/allocation/lifecycle rules are authored foreground methodology rather than invented source facts.
