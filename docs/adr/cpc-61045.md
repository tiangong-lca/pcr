# CPC 3.0 61045 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

Official leaf 61045 is wholesale trade services, not on a fee or contract basis, of miscellaneous household utensils, cutlery, crockery, glassware, china and pottery. The [UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), printed page 303, personally read, include class 3722 ceramic household and ornamental articles. The actual normalized 37221 tableware leaf was independently checked. Proposed `pcr.own-account-finished-glazed-porcelain-plate-wholesale` is narrower: manufacturer-finished fired/glazed feldspar-porcelain plates with inherited packing, merchant own-account ambient stock/handling and actual business-buyer accepted consignment.

## Semantic boundary

One Count reference is the actual unique completed commercial service, qualified by manufacturer/SKU/size/glaze/source lot and finished gate, plate-piece versus stack/carton membership, body-plus-glaze net mass versus separator/carton/support tare, real intact/chipped/broken condition, periods and buyer criteria. It is not one plate, shard, carton, kilogram, invoice or financial margin. A product-page packaging weight does not independently prove plate net weight.

Exclude raw materials/body preparation/forming/firing/glazing/finishing, merchant plate washing/sanitation/food-contact or strength testing, repair/grinding/repacking, unreviewed dedicated climate intervention, other tableware/materials, agency/retail and standalone logistics. Actual contracted extra processing remains within physical route review. Supplier-fired/glazed state and ordinary ambient holding do not establish a new preservation intervention. Four system-condition modules address commercial cohorts, crossed stock/resource periods, actual rejects/returns/breakage and complete-life shared infrastructure.

The closest ceramic tableware producer and household-utensil merchant directories were read directly: both are empty title-only scaffolds without material methodology. The existing material ceramic-floor-tiling PCR's actual scope, identity, boundary and process map were read; it measures a ready-substrate installed-area contractor service involving cutting, thinset bonding and grout. That physical gate and material treatment differ from untouched finished-plate merchant Count service. No product prose or cutting/installation emission duties were transplanted. Independent plate/pack/genealogy and source-gate/all-exit attribution needs justify this semantic identity rather than a classification gap.

All complete plate/body/glaze and inherited-pack manufacturing burdens, including lost commodity embodied upstream, remain outside the service add-on. The separate finished-plate commodity baseline covers every actual raw/body/forming/firing/glazing/finishing/packing stage once; raw clay/feldspar alone is incomplete. Record original source quantity/native state/unit/source and use documented direct source-to-target assignment, or compatible complete-cohort source quantity times target exit driver divided by all exit drivers. Native driver units/bases/sources/conversions and original-plate equivalence must be evidenced. Other useful exits, returns, closing and actual loss routes have exclusive assignments; an accepted-only denominator or shard count cannot allocate original plate demand. Actual compatible irreversible loss adjustment, sole owner and existing-loss coverage are reconciled once outside service. Unknown count/mass/condition differences are unresolved rather than waste, species, loss or default zero.

Actual discarded whole/chipped identifiable plates and associated pack transferred together differ from separately collected fragments, damaged packing, mixed hygiene solids and contained cleaning liquid. Parent-plate genealogy keeps fragments from becoming extra plate units. Body/glaze/component/retained-fluid analyses are nonadditive, with native quantity/unit/basis/method/source/sample time as relevant. Whole and component physical transfers are exclusive and receiver classification/treatment owned once. Chipping/breakage, captured fines or glaze composition alone does not establish airborne silica, metal leachate or other actual species/medium release. No assumed recycling/material substitution benefit is introduced.

The actual [IKEA 365+ plate product body](https://www.ikea.com/sg/en/p/ikea-365-plate-white-50258945/) was read as primary manufacturer technical material. It identifies a specific feldspar-porcelain/glaze product and separate packaging information. Neither numeric diameter/package mass nor consumer first-use washing, microwave/dishwasher or durability marketing is a universal warehouse operation, strength/breakage rate, lifespan or food-safety guarantee. Stock/allocation/lifecycle rules are authored foreground methods, not invented source claims.
