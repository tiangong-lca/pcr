## Semantic boundary

Existing exposed qualified unarmoured/unshielded polymeric multicore local LV electricity-distribution cable, compression straight joint and factory-paired two-part polyurethane pouch/mould, within actual approved rating up to1kV. New local cable asset and building copper-device/UTP first-installation PCRs do not provide this repair/fully-cured acceptance methodology.

Authorised isolation/prove-dead and safe continued control; actual cable/core/CSA/connector/die/crimp and kit/pouch/lot qualification, mould seals/mixing/fill, actual cure conditions and full required cured-state criterion, applicable calibrated electrical tests, defect closure and signed whole-joint handover with explicit energisation/backfill interfaces. Fully cured handover is this subset, not a universal prohibition on other approved earlier energisation.

The complete included and excluded scope is in the original bilingual PCR sections2–6. This is a reviewed repair subset, not coverage of every product, route or technology in the leaf.
