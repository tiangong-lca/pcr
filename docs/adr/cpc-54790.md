# CPC 3.0 54790 mapping decision

This individual decision records the reviewed semantic membership. The source classification label is **Other building completion and finishing services**. Proposed target: `pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.construction-services.other-building-completion-and-finishing-services`. Proposed relation: `narrower`.

The UN CPC 3.0 explanatory notes (30 June 2025), p. 298, explicitly include applying acoustic panels or tiles to interior walls and ceilings within 54790 and exclude vibration insulation. Source: [official explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf). The complete material index was screened for acoustic, ceiling and insulation records: the adjacent stone-wool wall-frame insulation methodology has a different material, physical interface and thermal cavity route and is not a duplicate of this acoustic-island installation service. Legacy classification scaffolds do not establish equivalent material methodology.

The proposed PCR covers first installation of one factory-finished glass-wool acoustic island, horizontal and uncut, with four matched adjustable wire suspensions and absorber anchors attached to previously accepted structural fixing points. It includes receipt/unpacking, panel marking, absorber-anchor fitting, temporary support/lifting, wire engagement, supported adjustment, leveling, retention/layout/depth/clearance inspection and signed acceptance. Reference output is one accepted installation service (`1 Item(s)`), not a supplied panel, assembly mass, treated room area or room acoustic result.

[Ecophon IG402 2024.11 / Solo Square M402](https://www.ecophon.com/en/products/free-hanging-units-and-baffles/solo/solo-square/) alternative 1 was visually reviewed in pages 1 and 3–9. Only its single horizontal panel sequence is selected. Direct-bracket, one-point, inclined, stacked, cut-out and added-load alternatives are excluded; their torque and geometry must not be imported. Structural-anchor construction, full ceilings, subsequent use/maintenance/removal and demonstrated occupied-room acoustic improvement remain outside the event. [Solo Square specification](https://www.ecophon.com/globalassets/media/pdf-and-documents/specification-texts/pdf/solo/solo-square.pdf) supports glass-wool finished-panel identity and secured hook/anchor retention, without establishing installed package mass or universal room performance.

The candidate retains unresolved concrete flow UUIDs after bounded identity lookup. Verified Number-of-items and Mass support chains are retained separately; generic Energy and standalone Duration support chains remain unresolved. No quantitative mass, energy, labor-time, waste or emission range is invented. Foreground protocols require actual quantities, shared-asset consumers/periods and burden ownership, reject/return/waste disposition, measured geometry and complete handover evidence.
