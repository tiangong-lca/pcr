## Semantic boundary

- Included products and market state: actual own-account live domestic Bos-cattle consignment wholesale through a temporary merchant-owned assembly/depot, with animal IDs, actual headcount/class/state/weight basis, welfare/care/fitness/time and commercial business-buyer acceptance.
- Excluded adjacent products: other animal species; breeding/rearing/fattening/pasture production; planned milking/calving; slaughter; animal-product manufacturing; agency/auction fees; retail; standalone storage-only care. Actual unforeseen birth/milk output requires explicit boundary/stock amendment, not assumed zero.
- Production route and handover gate: merchant ownership and resale risk, actual receipt condition and identity, temporary care/feed/water/segregation, actual competent fitness/commercial accepted handover to business buyer. Short-stay is a trade purpose, never a default number of hours or head-weight.
- Why the relation is supported, and any partial-coverage limit:61014 includes live animals, while this PCR is only a cattle dealer-depot route. Existing cattle-production PCR ends at farm-gate commodity output with rearing/fattening responsibilities, not this counted merchant service. Distinct methodology is individual ID/time/head stock, actual short-term care, enteric/manure systems and original-gate commodity loss interface. All traded-animal production including lost-head production is outside service, with actual production-only loss demand linked once; actual disposal/treatment remains service coverage. No claim to cover every live animal or cattle route.
