# Hold two blockboard CPC associations

The repository owner explicitly directed that CPC 3.0 31422 and 31423 mapping acceptance be held pending classification adjudication. Both substantive semantic PCRs remain in the material catalog. The frozen denominator remains 115, with 62 completed targets and these two targets awaiting classification review. The retained PCR count is 64; it is not the accepted-target count.

Remove only these two positive edges from the current mapping. Preserve their complete prior acceptance objects as historical evidence in [the hold assessment](../reviews/cpc3-115-20261106/classification-holds-20261110.json). Normal catalog generation consequently reports these leaves as unmapped and classification resolution selects no PCR. This decision does not assign the products to 31412 or 31413, remove a target, replace a semantic PCR, or alter the 1,451 pre-existing accepted edges.

The tropical product has four Meranti veneer sheets around one purchased Falcata solid-strip core; the temperate product has four birch veneer sheets around one pine/spruce solid-strip core. Both use alternating physical layer axes. The official 31412/31413 notes expressly include blockboard and allow a central core of another material, so the solely-sheet branch title cannot by itself exclude these constructions. The 31422/31423 notes also describe crossed multi-veneer panels with the relevant outer ply. These overlapping conditions require a maintained classification decision; the prior author/controller interpretation is not an official classification ruling.

The counterevidence is readable in the [CPC3 explanatory notes,30June2025, PDF pages139–140](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf#page=139), and the official [31412](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/31412), [31413](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/31413), [31422](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/31422) and [31423](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Structure/Detail/EN/2100/31423) records. Short source excerpts and applicability limits are preserved in the PR repair evidence.

This supersedes the two mapping acceptances in [batch03](2026-11-06-accept-scoped-cpc3-115-batch-03.md). It preserves the scoped methodology/translation review, the candidate/authored lifecycle, and the absence of independent scientific approval, actual study qualification and publication. Reinstatement requires a new explicit decision that addresses the actual construction and complete official notes, with counterevidence and validation tied to the resulting delivery source.
