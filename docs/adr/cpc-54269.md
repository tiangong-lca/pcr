# CPC 3.0 54269 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official leaf: General construction services of other industrial plants. Official [CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), printed pages 283 and 287, include construction, repair and alteration of iron foundries and distinguish ordinary industrial buildings.
- Target: `pcr.constructions-and-construction-services.construction-services.general-construction-services-of-other-industrial-plants`.
- Proposed relation: `narrower`. Classification is context and does not create semantic PCR identity.

## Semantic boundary

The target is one completed integrated reconstruction and commissioning intervention on a retained ferrous foundry coreless-induction melting plant. It includes actual necessary civil foundation, pit and melt-deck alterations, integrated furnace/power/control/hydraulic/charging/cooling/extraction interfaces, required first lining/dryout/sintering, supervised actual hot trial heats, correction/retest and signed owner/contractor acceptance under adopted project-specific criteria. Retained assets/heel are historical stocks; actual current charge, unused unchanged returns, useful historical equipment, processed trial-metal outlets, slag, captured dust, contained wastes and actual species/medium releases remain distinct.

The existing other-constructions-for-manufacturing record covers a new specialized physical asset with cold testing and excludes operational emissions and feedstocks. It does not silently supply the retained reconstruction stock accounting and necessary first-lining/hot-heat attribution here. Ordinary industrial buildings exclude foundries and later repair. Single-trade services remain upstream inputs, with actual scope boundaries. The new record is justified by these methods and gates, not by a different service noun or an unmapped CPC leaf.

Exclude a new whole foundry, generic buildings, nonferrous-only and blast/cupola/EAF-only routes, separate machine supply/erection, isolated relining/routine maintenance, cold-only installation and ordinary post-handover commercial metal production. Deliberately commercial co-production requires a separate production boundary and explicit justified attribution before concrete data. This narrow route does not cover all industrial plants in the source leaf. Supplier examples provide no universal capacities, counts, chemistry, yield, energy, acceptance or life values.
