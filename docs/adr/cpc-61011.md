## Semantic boundary

- Included products and market state: completed ownership-based B2B consignment wholesale service for already-dry, already-clean non-seed wheat held/handled in commercial silos, with actual mass/moisture/quality/residence and accepted commercial gate.
- Excluded adjacent products: wheat growing/harvest, deliberate drying/primary conditioning/milling/feed manufacture, seed, other grain/oilseed/feed classes, fee/commission agency, retail and independently sold storage-only service. All traded-wheat upstream production, including loss-associated production, is outside this service add-on.
- Production route and handover gate: merchant ownership and risk; receipt checking and lot segregation; silo handling/surveillance/ambient aeration; accepted contracted resale handover to business buyer. A consignment is an actual documented transaction lot, not a fixed tonne, truckload, invoice split or monetary margin.
- Why the relation is supported, and any partial-coverage limit: owns and resells a grain product to a business buyer, within61011, but only the dry wheat silo route is covered. Existing wheat-other PCR ends at farm/primary-conditioning commodity gate; it has no own-account wholesale-service boundary, physical/commercial consignment count, loss-linked commodity interface or shared merchant-asset responsibility. This is a separate service methodology, not a copy of commodity production or a promise to cover every grain/oilseed/feed route in61011.
