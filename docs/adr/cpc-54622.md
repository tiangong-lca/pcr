## Semantic boundary

Six-inch rigid PVC DR35 gasket-jointed gravity sanitary exterior building lateral between a provided outside-building outlet and prepared authorized existing receiver on private untrafficked bare ground. Starting at a safe stable pre-excavated trench, the installer owns new offsite-supplied bedding/haunch/full backfill, manual gasket assembly, exposed-joint water test and concealment approval, complete layer compaction and agreed bare-ground grade before final signed buried-lateral acceptance.

Excluded: Interior pipe/vent networks; local-main or chamber construction/live tapping/bypass; excavation, separately owned temporaryworks and removed-soil handling; road/pavement restoration; reused excavated fill, other pipe/joint specifications or combustion equipment without reviewed scope change; later operation/treatment/maintenance.
