## Semantic boundary

Completed in-place hydraulic elevation and permanent foundation reconnection of a retained qualified timber-frame one- or two-dwelling house originally on a crawlspace foundation. Start with named structurally assessed existing house and engineered permitted lifting/flood-elevation and new/extended foundation/access/utility plan. Own actual disconnect, temporary supports/lifting beams/hydraulic jacks, foundation/necessary groundworks and curing, lowering/anchoring to permanent qualified foundation, permanent access stairs/landing and restored qualified utilities; actual removed foundation/debris/damage repair/temporary utilities and equipment owned. Exclude slab-lift/masonry-only/mobile homes, relocation to a new site, new house/reconstruction/additional dwelling, unfinished temporary elevation and future flood events/insurance/operation.

Actual route and handover gate: Competent project-specific structural/lift/foundation design, actual local flood datum/required elevation and loading; inspected permanent foundation/anchorage, final surveyed elevation, safe permanent egress/access, actual completed utility reconnection/test and defect closure/signed owner/authority handover. Historical FEMA guide supports physical route, not current law/default BFE/freeboard. No procedural lifting instructions or universal engineering dimensions.

Reference and comparability context: N>0 unique finally accepted physical house IDs. One house containing two dwellings counts once, not two outputs, floor area, jacks/lifts, lift height or temporary attempts. Existing retained house structure is baseline not newly purchased manufacture; actual incremental foundation/materials/access/utility works retained. Failed attempts, preparation, stock exits and all asset/provider resources stay in numerator.

Reuse assessment: Existing one/two-dwelling asset PCR hands over a new whole completed building and explicitly excludes separately sold construction services. It has no retained-house lift/temporary-support/permanent-foundation reconnection, actual flood datum and restored access/utilities handover. Scaffolding/piling/earthwork services cannot substitute this integrated accepted service.

This semantic record requires retained-asset intervention gates and resource/state attribution specific to this route. It does not claim every possible route in the classification leaf.
