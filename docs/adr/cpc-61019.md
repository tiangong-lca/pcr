# CPC 3.0 61019 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official leaf: Wholesale trade services, not on a fee or contract basis, of agricultural raw materials n.e.c.
- Source: [UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), printed page 300, explicitly includes trade services related to class `0195` natural rubber in primary forms or in plates, sheets or strip. Class `0192` fibre crops instead belongs to `61011` on page 299 and cannot establish this edge.
- Canonical target: `pcr.own-account-bulk-wholesale-of-preserved-natural-rubber-latex`.
- Proposed relation: `narrower`; only the supplier-released, already concentrated/preserved Hevea latex merchant bulk-tank wholesale subset, not every 61019 product or natural-rubber form.

## Semantic boundary

A merchant purchases and resells already concentrated and preserved natural latex from a supplier's final release gate, receives actual tank/lot quantities, performs representative quality verification, holds and transfers that same-grade material using electric equipment, and completes an actual business-buyer accepted bulk consignment. One Count reference is a unique accepted commercial service, with actual wet quantity, DRC/TSC, preservative/quality state, sampling, holding period and gate qualifiers. It is not one tanker, dry tonne, standard concentration, invoice or monetary margin.

Exclude plantation/tapping/collection, concentration, preservative addition, deliberate maturation or grade adjustment, dilution, blending grades, compounding, coagulation/recovery processing, vulcanization and finished articles; fee agency, retail and standalone storage/transport are also excluded. Outsourcing an actual material-changing operation does not remove it from the route and requires separate reviewed scope. Actual tank transfer/sampling and cleaning consume measured service resources without importing a supplier's formulation recipe or universal storage schedule.

The existing `natural-rubber-in-primary-forms-or-in-plates-sheets-or-strip` material PCR covers managed rubber trees, tapping and exactly one primary-processing route through a primary-processing hand-off, including concentration/stabilization for its latex route. It does not define this downstream merchant-only commercial reference or merchandise-loss interface. This new semantic identity is justified by the post-release ownership/gate and independent physical service and quality/stock attribution duties, not by a classification gap.

All traded-latex manufacturing burdens, including losses' embodied upstream, stay outside this service add-on. Its non-exchange ledger records actual whole-liquid mass, DRC dry rubber and TSC analytical views with representative samples, tank heels, sample removals, return/reject/spill/residue and cleaning fates. Those views are not additive. TSC minus DRC is only a compatible analytical difference, not chemical speciation; one minus TSC is not proved pure water, and inherited preservative concentration is not emitted ammonia. Actual direct species require independent measurement or justified matched activity/composition/fate. Actual compatible original-gate loss adjustment, quantity/unit/basis/source, existing-loss coverage and sole commodity-system owner are required exactly once outside the service before complete-chain use. Service loss-handling and receiving treatment remain physically covered once.

ISO 123/126/124 public scopes support appropriate sampling/DRC/TSC distinctions and applicability limits, not inaccessible laboratory procedures or universal commercial thresholds. THAITEX primary route and specification distinguish supplier concentration/preservation/final adjustment and separate assays; its numeric product criteria and process examples are not PCR defaults. Stock, asset, provider and normalization rules are declared foreground methodology, not claims drawn from supplier quantity examples.
