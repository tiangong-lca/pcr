# CPC 3.0 61033 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

Official leaf 61033 is wholesale trade services, not on a fee or contract basis, of articles of clothing, articles of fur and clothing accessories. The [UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), printed page 302, include group 282 wearing apparel. Proposed target `pcr.own-account-folded-wool-knit-jumper-wholesale` is narrower: manufacturer-finished folded/packed wool-containing knitted jumpers under merchant ownership, ordinary ambient handling/stock responsibilities and actual business-buyer acceptance.

## Semantic boundary

One Count reference is the unique completed commercial service, with actual style/size/SKU/source lot, factory-finished pack gate, wool/blend composition and basis/source, piece count, whole garment net mass including integral trims/internal labels, external packing/support tare, component moisture method/basis/sample/time/representativeness, actual condition/periods and buyer criteria. One service is not one jumper, wool kilogram, invoice or financial margin. Wool naming does not establish 100% wool or allow dry textile composition to multiply whole as-received garment mass blindly.

Exclude wool/garment production, washing/steam/ironing, repair/redye, garment chemical/pest treatment, manufacturer primary opening/repacking and dedicated climate intervention without review, other clothing/fur, fee agency, retail and standalone warehousing/transport. Non-invasive or representative traceable supplier measurements may support facts without compulsory destructive opening. Ordinary ambient holding and inherited folding/pack state do not establish a new preservation intervention. Four system-condition modules cover consignment operation, crossed stocks/periods, actual rejection/returns and full-life shared assets.

The existing greasy shorn-wool producer's actual full relevant scope, identity, boundary and profile were read. It ends at unscoured producing-farm fleece and excludes post-shear scouring/topmaking/yarn; it cannot cover finished apparel or this commercial service reference. The closest 28226 knitted-apparel directory is a title-only empty scaffold, without material methodology. Independent style/size/blend/whole-garment-trim/moisture/condition and factory-gate attribution duties justify a new semantic merchant target. No product prose was copied.

All actual wool/blend-fibre and garment manufacturing/finishing/inherited-pack stages, including lost material's embodied upstream, stay outside this add-on. The separate finished/packed commodity system must cover every actual missing post-farm scouring/topmaking/spinning/knitting/assembly/finishing/fold/pack stage and other blend production once. Quantify actual original-gate input and assigned demand in compatible state/unit/composition/moisture basis with source/allocation/conversion, including evidenced ordinary state-change share and exclusive returns/closing/other exits. Actual compatible loss adjustment has one external owner and existing-loss coverage. Neither final dispatch plus waste nor unknown mass/count difference alone establishes source demand, loss or emission.

Shape distortion or visible/supplier insect/mould observations differ from actual return, retained rejection and real discard. Actual discarded garment stream includes associated pack only when transferred together, excluding duplicate separate packing output. Mixed hygiene residues/liquids and captured fibres are distinct contained states; actual direct species/medium/particle identity requires independent evidence. Blend labels or consumer pest advice do not prove chemical/biological/microfibre release. Real service cleanup/receiver treatment has one owner.

[Woolmark's storage page](https://www.woolmark.com/care/how-to-store-wool-clothes/) was read directly as primary industry consumer technical material. It supports qualitative folding/shape/moisture/insect context. Household clean-first, vacuum/new bags, silica/cedar/mothballs and household inspection timing are not wholesale requirements, inventory defaults, empirical loss factors or releases. Generic stock/lifecycle and composition-conversion rules are authored foreground methodology rather than unsupported source claims.
