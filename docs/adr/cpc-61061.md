## Semantic boundary

One Count reference is an actual completed commercial service at final business-buyer acceptance after actual reversals. Explicit merchant identity/ownership-resale evidence, business-buyer identity, acceptance gate/source and reversal records are required; supplier receipt alone proves neither own-account nor B2B. Original sheet/stack/pack/rack IDs, grade/state, geometry/count/area/net glass mass/density basis, interleave/pack/support tare, actual condition/ambient/hold times and buyer criteria qualify service. It is not one sheet, rack, shard, m2, kilogram or margin. Only actual positive final N permits normalization; open/rejected/reversed resources retain real event/period attribution without fictional service count.

Exclude merchant cutting/edge working/grinding/polishing/toughening/lamination/coating/glass washing/stain remediation/installation, coated/laminated/toughened/ground-polished routes, unreviewed dedicated climate intervention, fee agency/retail and standalone storage. Extra actual contracted work remains a route extension. Supplier finished state/interleaving and ordinary ambient observation do not themselves establish operated preservation. Four system-condition modules cover real consignment/cohorts, crossperiod stocks/resources, actual rejects/returns and complete shared infrastructure.

Closest actual37113 producer and61061 merchant manifest/full English reads show empty title-only scaffolds without usable equivalent method; material index search found no flat/float/sheet-glass methodology. Independent original-sheet geometry/density/pack/genealogy, condition/source-gate and exclusive receiver accounting justify this semantic boundary, rather than classification incompleteness or copied product prose.

Actual [Pilkington ATS-104 Protecting Flat Glass Surfaces,2013](https://www.pilkington.com/-/media/pilkington/site-content/usa/window-manufacturers/technical-bulletins/ats104protectingflatglasssurfaces20130114.pdf), all four pages, and [manufacturer float product body](https://www.pilkington.com/en-GB/united-kingdom/architectural-and-technical-glass/product-categories/thermal-insulation/pilkington-optifloat-clear) were directly read. They support qualitative actual storage-condition and product-versus-further-processing questions only. No universal numeric humidity/hold/density/strength/size/lifetime/recycling/compliance guarantee, cleaning chemical/etchant/solvent/remediation recipe or species release is adopted. Actual uncoated annealed supplied state and process records govern; book chemical examples do not become service inputs or emissions. Equations/stock/allocation/asset rules are explicit foreground collection methods, not claims that manufacturer publications prescribe them.

All actual glass raw-material/melting/float/annealing/supplier cut-to-original-supply-size and inherited interleave/pack upstream, including embodied actual lost commodity, remains outside service. Raw silica/cullet alone or an unmatched fabricated downstream product is inadequate. Quantify original source demand via evidenced direct mapping, or complete compatible source cohort times target exit driver divided by a positive complete all-terminal-exit denominator. Native units/bases/methods/sources/owners and original-sheet equivalence include closing stock, useful other exits, returns and actual loss without duplication. Final dispatch mass plus waste is not original demand; unknown stain/break/mass residual is unresolved rather than loss/discard/recycling/species/zero.

Actual width/height/thickness/shape/area/count have native units, each-total scope and conversion sources. Geometric mass uses actual representative thickness and density/state/method/source/sample time/temperature-unit and uncertainty; actual weighed net glass differs from a derived estimate. No universal density or fragment count reconstructs original sheets. Original-sheet→fragment genealogy, whole sheet/fragments/separate pack/mixed cleanup/contained liquid and retained moisture/component/fluid views remain nonadditive, with actual receiver classification/treatment ownership once. Captured glass is not an airborne particle; composition/stain is not actual species or receptor. Useful transfers introduce no automatic substitution benefit.

Freight cargo retains actual carried interleave/pack/rack supports while subtracting only noncargo truck/container tare. EACH quantity multiplies compatible actual count once; TOTAL does not. Native work, physical share and N apply once. Physical support cargo is distinct from new consumed material or duplicated lifecycle burden. Full assets cover all-user observed history plus sourced remaining use and actual lifecycle/EOL with native driver evidence and uncertainty. Supply stock uses gross input plus separate actual matched unused outward return, or consumed-only input omitting its paired output.
