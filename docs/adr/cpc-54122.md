## Semantic boundary

The included product is a completed integrated landlord Category A base-building services/basic-finishes refurbishment within a surveyed retained office footprint, ready for later tenant fit-out under an actual contract. Selective stripout/hazard clearance releases retained substrates; actual floor/ceiling/basic finish roles and electrical/lighting/HVAC/wet/fire interfaces are integrated, tested/retested and defect-closed before landlord acceptance. The sole denominator is unique accepted net internal office area A in m2 under a declared survey convention. Associated common/core and retained-system work stays causally attributed; trade areas, tests and intermediate transfers do not enlarge A.

Exclude a new whole building, extension/new floor area, structural rebuilding, use conversion, tenant Cat B bespoke partitions/furniture/IT/equipment and future operation. Cat A is a practice label, not a universal specification or tenant occupation-readiness certificate. The WDI Cat A/B explanation supports this distinction. WDI Riverside House supports phased occupied-site and common-service interfaces, but its extensions/new area and Cat B are outside this PCR. Its project dimensions, zero-valued webpage statistics and BREEAM rating provide no quantitative defaults.

The existing commercial-buildings PCR ends at accepted new whole-building asset handover and does not own the surveyed retained-office intervention starting state. The existing apartment integrated energy-retrofit PCR concerns a whole-building counted residential envelope/heat-pump/HRV/PV gate. Separate finishes, cables, raised floor or HVAC work can be upstream inputs but cannot substitute for coordinated landlord Cat A completion.
