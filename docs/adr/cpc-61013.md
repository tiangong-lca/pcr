# CPC 3.0 61013 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

- Official leaf: Wholesale trade services, not on a fee or contract basis, of unmanufactured tobacco. Source: [UNSD CPC 3.0 structure](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Structure_30Jun2025.csv), retained normalized leaf `61013`.
- Canonical target: `pcr.own-account-wholesale-of-baled-cured-tobacco-leaf`.
- Proposed relation: `narrower`. Only one declared merchant-own-account, electrically handled, intact-baled already-cured-leaf wholesale route is covered; the complete classification leaf is not a methodology recipe.

## Semantic boundary

One commercial service buys and resells traceable intact already-cured baled leaf at one named wholesale warehouse, with receiving verification, stock tracking, monitored storage, electric handling and business-buyer accepted consignment handover. The reference is one actual complete accepted consignment with actual mass, tobacco type, curing history, grade, moisture basis, package, time, site and gate qualifiers. It is not a standard weight, bale, invoice, fee, margin or a tobacco product-flow UUID.

Exclude farming, harvest, curing, redrying, reconditioning, deliberate humidification, fermentation/aging as a processing purpose, stemming, blending, tobacco manufacture, fee agency, retail, storage-only and transport-only services. Ancillary pest packages cover surveillance/sanitation/building operations that do not treat traded leaf. Outsourcing direct leaf fumigation or material-changing treatment does not remove the physical operation from the route: actual occurrence requires a separately reviewed extension.

The existing material `unmanufactured-tobacco` PCR concerns cultivation and harvest with optional curing/grade/packing to farm or curing hand-off. It does not cover this commercial own-account service gate or service-only merchandise/commodity-loss interface. The new identity is justified by wholesale responsibility, stock/moisture/reject/return records, physical service output and full-life shared warehouse attribution, not by the existence of an unmapped code. The retained leaf scaffold is not promoted or silently followed through its legacy alias.

This is strictly a service add-on. Every traded-merchandise manufacturing burden, including the embodied upstream of lost tobacco, stays outside its exchanges. The non-exchange commodity ledger must quantify compatible-basis actual losses, fates, linked commodity dataset/gate, adjustment quantity/basis/unit/source and one responsible owner. Before complete-chain or final-package readiness, the linked commodity system must include the measured loss-related upstream burden exactly once. Service resources and actual loss handling/treatment stay within the physical wholesale scope. The PCR does not equate moisture change or a stock residual with leaf destruction or atmospheric release, classify every commercial scrap outlet as waste, or grant automatic avoided-product credit.

INRAE storage guidance supports only qualitative stored-bale conditions and hygiene context. USDA tobacco sources support actual grade context and the distinction between commercial scrap and disposed material; U.S. grade injury/waste tolerances are not universal wholesale loss factors. These sources do not supply the PCR's declared stock/asset/normalization methodology or empirical default quantities. Full current CORESTA guidance was not accessible; unseen warehouse detail is not cited as evidence.
