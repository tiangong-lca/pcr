## Semantic boundary

A merchant purchases and resells inspected, slaughtered and completed-primary-chilled bovine bone-in half carcasses, operates hanging cold-room preservation and handling, and completes an actual business-buyer accepted commercial consignment. One Count reference is the unique completed service, qualified by actual carcass identities, net body quantity after measured hooks/supports/pack tare, condition/temperature criteria, ownership, periods and buyer gate. It is not one half carcass, kilogram, invoice, standard storage duration or monetary margin.

Exclude livestock production, slaughter/dressing/primary chilling, cutting/deboning/mincing/cooking/freezing/thawing and deliberate ageing programmes, agency, retail and standalone storage/transport. Record ordinary natural postmortem changes and actual times/conditions rather than presuming zero change. Actual maintenance of already chilled goods cannot hide incomplete primary cooling. Outsourced material-changing processing requires separate route review.

Closest existing live-cattle producer methodology ends before slaughter and cannot define this commercial preservation/handover reference. Existing wholesale siblings concern different commodity and preservation/stock duties. The independent need is a merchant-owned cold preservation boundary, source-gate carcass state and traceable body/tare/condition, cold-room resource attribution and specific refrigerant/drip interfaces; no product prose was copied.

All commodity production burdens, including embodied upstream of lost carcass material, remain outside this service add-on. The separate original-gate system must cover cattle production, slaughter/dressing and completed primary chilling through supplier release, exactly once. Trace actual source-gate input and quantity assigned to each accepted cohort, including evidenced ordinary moisture/state-change share; final dispatch body mass plus discard cannot replace original-gate demand. Returns, closing and other exits are assigned exclusively. Actual irreversible loss additionally requires compatible original-gate quantity/state/unit/source, sole owner and existing-loss coverage to avoid duplicate adjustment. Service contains real loss handling and receiver/treatment responsibilities once. Drip, evaporation or unresolved shrink do not automatically equal edible-meat loss or direct pollutant species.

New refrigerant supply, initial factory charge, installed stock, actual recovered useful export, retained/recharged recovery, dedicated unusable fluid waste and independently confirmed direct releases remain distinct. Initial charge already owned by an asset dataset cannot be deducted from unrelated current make-up on export. Matching standalone supply-stock return conventions are explicit. Complete cold-room/rail/handling/refrigeration asset life covers all consumers, actual periods and sourced remainder with one burden owner.

Historical [FAO cold preservation](https://www.fao.org/4/t0098e/t0098e02.htm) and [cold-store planning](https://www.fao.org/4/t0098e/t0098e03.htm) support the route/state and hanging-rail context. No historical numeric temperatures, durations, densities, cleaning recipes, loss/leak factors or present-law/shelf-life claims are adopted. Generic stock and lifecycle attribution rules are authored foreground methodology rather than claims drawn from those manuals.
