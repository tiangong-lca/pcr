# CPC 3.0 61042 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

Official CPC 3.0 leaf 61042 covers wholesale trade services, not on a fee or contract basis, of radio and television equipment and recorded audio and video disks and tapes. The [UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), printed page 303, include group 473 television receivers. The personally read source also lists class 4721 and groups 474/476. Proposed target `pcr.own-account-boxed-unpowered-lcd-television-wholesale` is narrower: factory-boxed unpowered LCD televisions with LED backlights, merchant own-account ordinary ambient holding/handling and actual B2B accepted commercial gate.

## Semantic boundary

One Count reference is one unique completed commercial service. Actual model/serial/SKU/region, complete factory release and boxed unpowered state, real supplied configuration, stand/remote/adapter/cables/cells only if actually included, TV piece count, whole merchandise/pack mass basis, condition, periods and buyer criteria qualify it. It is not a TV piece, bare panel, carton, invoice, financial margin or consumer operation. Actual unpowered buyer acceptance may rely on traceable manufacturer/pack records without inventing a powered functional test.

Exclude panel/component/receiver manufacture, merchant activation/demo/electrical testing/software update, repair/disassembly/refurbishment, stand assembly, mounting/installation, routine primary opening/reboxing, other display technologies, unreviewed dedicated climate intervention, agency/retail and standalone logistics. Actual contracted processing remains real and requires separate review. Inherited box/unpowered state and ordinary ambient holding do not establish a preservation intervention; four system-condition modules address commercial cohorts, crossed stock/periods, rejection/returns and complete-life shared assets.

The closest TV-receiver producer and CPC-linked merchant directories were read directly in full; both are title-only empty scaffolds without material methodology. Existing merchant siblings do not cover the actual model/serial/configuration, complete receiver versus panel/stand/accessory/box and unpowered-gate duties. Independent physical merchant needs justify the semantic target, rather than a classification leaf alone. No product prose was copied.

All actual receiver, panel/backlight/electronics/enclosure, included stand/accessories/remote/cells/adapter/cables, assembly/factory testing and inherited packing burdens, including embodied upstream of losses, remain outside the service add-on. A bare panel or PCB dataset is incomplete. The separate commodity system must cover all missing actual stages once. Quantify compatible complete factory-source input and assigned demand, actual source/native state/unit/allocation/conversion and exclusive returns/closing/other exits. Actual irrecoverable whole-unit/component loss adjustment has one external owner and existing-loss coverage. Stock residual, missing part or final dispatch plus waste alone is insufficient; unknown state is a gap rather than loss, release or default zero. Included cells/accessories are not newly consumed service supplies.

Whole kit, TV body with/without stand, bare panel and individual accessories are nonadditive views. Published masses are comparable only for the same actual model/configuration/state/method. A difference between guide figures does not establish stand mass or all included accessories. Actual discarded whole TV/kit/associated pack is distinct from physically separated parts/cells, separately contained incident materials, packing and mixed hygiene/cleaning liquid, with native quantities/bases/sources, parent/transfer IDs and receiver classification/treatment. No routine dismantling/battery-removal recipe, assumed leak chemistry or automatic salvage/recycling credit is introduced. Damage and component inventory alone establish no direct species/medium release.

The actual two-page [Sony KD-X75K/X74K family reference guide](https://pro.sony/support/res/manuals/5038/ac33ccb749a17b7a3391ebffd0210489/50381580M.pdf) was read independently. It supports a specific LCD/LED model example, stand/body and regional accessory distinctions and panel/carton transport context. Model weights/dimensions/person counts, installation/activation steps, rated/standby/annual consumer energy and the annual-use scenario are not universal service defaults. Actual received model/configuration and instructions govern the case. Generic stock/lifecycle and receiver rules are authored foreground methodology rather than unsupported guide claims.
