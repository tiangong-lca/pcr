## Semantic boundary

Permanent electric-arc single-mode single-fibre connection repair in an existing long-distance LAND telecom cable, with compatible retained ends/slack and a serviceable re-enterable sealed closure. Long-distance asset construction and indoor copper Cat6 installation do not provide this fusion/retained-closure repair methodology.

Mechanical and optical checks are separate. Calibrated optical readings at actual wavelength/owner criterion, accurate OTDR splice loss by matched bidirectional average, heat-shrink protection, bend-safe organiser routing, retained closure reseal/required checks, defect closure and signed bounded fibre-pair acceptance. Estimated splicer loss, sleeve count or closure count cannot establish N.

The complete included and excluded scope is in the original bilingual PCR sections2–6. This is a reviewed repair subset, not coverage of every product, route or technology in the leaf. Product facts and gate criteria come from current-route evidence; the CPC leaf supplies classification context only.
