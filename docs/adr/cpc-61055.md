## Semantic boundary

One Count reference is one actual completed commercial consignment service, not a bicycle piece, frame, wheel, pedal, carton, kilogram, invoice or margin. Actual supplier gate, manufacturer/model/frame serial or lot/size, nonmotorized configuration and partial assembly, supplied complete BOM including attached/expected detached wheel/pedal/accessory/tool membership, kit/carton counts, net/tare/content state and real acceptance/time/site/period qualify service. Complete supplied kit is different from final assembly or ride readiness. Expected detached parts are not automatically missing goods.

Exclude e-bikes/motorized cycles, other sporting goods/boats, loose aftermarket-parts trade, factory material/component manufacture/finishing/partial assembly/packing, merchant final assembly/fitting/pressure/torque adjustment/lubrication/PDI/ride/repair/washing/repacking, fee agency/retail and standalone storage. Extra actual contracted processing remains a route extension. Ambient holding and inherited packaging do not establish operated preservation or assembly modules. Four system-condition modules cover actual commercial cohorts, crossed stock/resource periods, rejects/returns and shared full-life assets.

Actual closest49921 producer manifest/full English and61055 sports merchant full English are empty title-only scaffolds; current material index contains no equivalent bicycle-kit merchant method. Independent actual partial-state/BOM and original received-gate accounting, EACH/TOTAL/cargo-tare transport and retained tire/lubricant/whole-part receiver distinctions justify the semantic identity. No predecessor product prose is copied or useful methodology inferred from classification coverage alone.

The actual declared nonmotorized partial kit and supplied BOM must be evidenced separately. Stock/allocation/lifecycle/transport equations are explicit foreground collection methods, not claims that the consumer page establishes them.

All actual material/frame/wheel/tire/drivetrain/accessory production, finish, manufacturer partial assembly/lubrication and inherited packing through the original packed PARTIAL gate, including lost commodity embodied upstream, remains outside service. A raw-parts basket or unmatched assembled-ready-to-ride/use-phase baseline is inadequate. Actual whole partial kit, independently transferred part, separated packaging, mixed cleanup solids and contained liquid require real receiver classification/acceptance and treatment ownership once. Inherited tires/lubricant/coating and retained contents remain commodity audit within actual whole-kit/part waste; independent components/physical transfers/direct species views cannot be added again. No product wear/ride/maintenance emission, current lubricant input or pressure/stock-loss-to-release factor is assumed. Native component/content quantity unit/basis/method/source/sample time and fate evidence are required.

Freight uses actual boxed kits plus shipped accessories/inherited or added pack and carried support, distinct from noncargo truck/container tare. EACH compatible mass is multiplied by actual matching count once; TOTAL stays total. Payload/distance conversions, transport work, causal service-share and N denominator each apply once. Actual reusable support in cargo does not become a new consumed input.
