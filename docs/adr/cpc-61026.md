# CPC 3.0 61026 mapping decision

## Source leaf and target

Official CPC 3.0 leaf 61026 is wholesale trade services, not on a fee or contract basis, of beverages. The [UNSD CPC 3.0 explanatory notes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/classifications/Econ/Download/In%20Text/CPC_Ver_3.0_Exp_Notes_30Jun2025.pdf), printed page 301, include group 244 soft drinks and bottled mineral waters. Proposed target `pcr.own-account-sealed-pet-mineral-water-wholesale` is narrower: merchant own-account wholesale of supplier-filled sealed one-way PET non-carbonated natural mineral water through ordinary ambient warehouse handling and actual business-buyer acceptance.

## Semantic boundary

One Count reference is one unique completed commercial consignment. Actual source/product/lot, non-carbonated mineral-water and filled-sealed PET state, bottle/case count and unit, labelled volume, actual contents quantity, density-temperature evidence, inherited bottle/closure/label/outer/support tare, ownership, periods and buyer conditions qualify it. It is not a fixed bottle count, litre, tonne, pallet turn, invoice or monetary margin.

Exclude source extraction, treatment, water/container manufacture and filling, opening/resealing/new carbonation, bulk/sparkling/flavoured/sweetened water and other drinks, returnable-bottle washing/refilling, dedicated climate intervention without fresh review, fee agency, retail and standalone warehousing/transport. Supplier seals and ordinary ambient storage do not establish a new operated preservation intervention. Four system-condition modules cover consignment/cohort operation, cross-period stocks/resources, reject/return/actual breach routes and all-consumer complete-lifecycle shared assets.

The closest bottled-water producer directory was read in full: it is an empty scaffold with a title only, without material methodology to reuse. Merchant siblings do not supply this product's contents/count/label-volume/pack-tare, intact/breached state and filled-sealed original-gate duties. New identity is justified by those physical service, state and attribution requirements rather than the classification leaf alone. No product prose was copied.

Water and inherited PET/closure/label/packing production and filling, including losses embodied upstream, remain outside the service. The separately linked commodity baseline must reach actual released filled/sealed product; an extraction-only water dataset is incomplete. Quantify assigned original-gate demand in compatible state/unit with source, allocation/conversion and exclusive returns/closing/other exits, including actual unrecovered originally supplied water and matching packaging where evidenced. Reconcile actual compatible loss adjustment and existing-loss coverage under one owner outside the service once. Stock deficit alone proves neither water loss nor waste nor emission.

Usable commercial returns, intact discarded filled packs, actual breach with separated packaging, mixed cleanup solids and contained liquids have disjoint physical fates and receiver/treatment owners. An intact wholepack waste transfer cannot also transfer its contained water and PET separately. Commodity mineral water is not purchased cleaning utility, direct abstraction or automatic pure-water discharge. Actual species and receiving medium require independent evidence; non-carbonated does not imply chemically zero dissolved carbon dioxide. Actual warehouse cleaning, handling and waste treatment remain covered once. Reusable pallets and full warehouse/handling assets cover complete life and all users rather than new manufacture on every circulation.

Historical official [Codex hygienic practice, revised 2011](https://www.fao.org/input/download/standards/224/CXP_033e.pdf), section 8, supports packaged distribution exposure concerns, and [Codex mineral-water standard, amended 2011](https://www.fao.org/input/download/standards/223/CXS_108e.pdf), sections 2.2.2 and 5, supports non-carbonated/hermetic state distinctions. No historic numerical limit, default density/chemical recipe, health claim or present global legal-compliance assertion is adopted. Generic stock/allocation/lifecycle methods are authored foreground requirements rather than numeric source claims.
