## Semantic boundary

The PCR covers site friction-fitting of declared unfaced semi-rigid mineral stone wool slabs into an accepted dry, stable, open wall frame. It begins with the accepted existing-frame interface, measures actual clear apertures, cuts supplied slabs, installs them by friction fit, inspects and corrects work, performs local cleanup and hands over accepted insulation before enclosure. Reference quantity is 1 m2 net accepted insulated cavity area, with slab grade, thickness, density and conductivity basis declared.

Frame manufacture/construction/major repair, lining, membranes, exterior render systems, insulation manufacture, glass wool, foams, blown/sprayed insulation, demolition and asbestos work are excluded. Stone wool is 岩棉; asbestos is 石棉 and is excluded. Material conductivity does not establish whole-wall thermal performance.

The CPC leaf covers thermal insulation services more generally, so this particular material, form, installation route and handover gate support a narrower relation. This PCR does not claim complete methodology coverage of every route within the leaf.
